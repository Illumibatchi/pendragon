## Where we left off (end of [[Session 040 - The Breach at Jagent|Session 040]])
The siege of [[Castle Jagent]] has ended in blood and sorcery. [[Liam]] breached the wall with an explosive barrel at the drain, the company charged the breach, and [[Drustin]] killed [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] in single combat. With Erof dead, the defenders surrendered.

[[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] has emerged from Jagent with [[Ygraine]] and proclaimed a new era of peace, justice, and security for his empire. Whatever [[Merlin]] did to him in the forest ritual remains unclear, and Merlin himself vanished in the magical darkness around the stone circle.

[[Millicent]] still has the sealed black vial given at the bridge: black as soot, wax-sealed, and tagged with two yellow dragons of Uther/Logres. Its intended use is unknown.

Far away, while Uther was occupied with Cornwall, [[King Ælle of Sussex|Ælle]] and [[Cissa]] took [[Pevensey]] after starvation and slaughtered its inhabitants as sacrifices to Wotan.
