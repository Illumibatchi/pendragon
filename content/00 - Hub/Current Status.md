## Where we left off (end of [[Session 030 - The Angel’s Command and the Child King|Session 030]])
The company has carried the March through its immediate crisis. At [[Lambor Castle]], [[Duke Edaris of the March]] was nearly spent, but [[Sir Madoc]]’s claimed angelic mission led the company to [[Peterborough]], where they captured [[King Wihtlaeg of the Angles]] alive rather than killing him. The Angle offensive stalled for the rest of 489, though skirmishing continued in the north.

The refugee crisis was handled without disaster: [[Drustin]] and [[Lady Rosalyn]] brought a refugee train safely into [[Salisbury]], while [[Sir Geraint]] secured aid for another group at [[Carohaig]] from [[Leodegrance]]. Rosalyn and Drustin have begun a courtship.

By year’s end, [[Millicent]] and [[Sir Madoc]] conceived a daughter, [[Meredith (daughter of Madoc and Millicent)|Meredith]]. In spring 490, [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] arrived with [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] and a combined army. Through [[Merlin]]’s urging, Uther and Cornwall have reconciled and now intend to march north against the Saxons.

## Immediate fallout
- **Spring 490 northern campaign:** Uther and Cornwall are marching north together against the Saxons.
- **Wihtlaeg captured alive:** the child King of the Angles is a living prisoner, not a martyr.
- **Madoc recovering but conflicted:** Madoc chose mercy and seems more himself, but remains troubled by the angel’s command and what it meant.
- **Rosalyn / Drustin courtship:** Rosalyn’s mercy and Drustin’s refugee rescue have opened a new courtship thread.
- **Meredith conceived:** Millicent and Madoc now have a daughter/heir on the way.
- **Famine/manors still matter:** the company’s Salisbury manors remain a pressure point after the bad winter and campaigning.
