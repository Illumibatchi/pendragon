## Where we left off (end of [[Session 031 - The Battle of Lincoln and the Birth of Meredith|Session 031]])
[[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] and [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] marched north together after [[Merlin]]’s reconciliation and defeated Saxon forces at the [[Lincoln|Battle of Lincoln]]. The conroi survived a brutal day: they captured a high-value enemy commander, killed a small giant and its wild-men escort, and were nearly overwhelmed by elite dismounted Saxon knights.

[[Millicent]] rode into battle heavily pregnant in polished maternity armor, was unhorsed, and gave birth on the battlefield to [[Meredith (daughter of Madoc and Millicent)|Meredith]]. [[Sir Madoc]] survived grave wounds and recurring madness long enough to see his daughter born. Uther has called a feast to celebrate both the victory and the birth of his granddaughter.

## Immediate fallout
- **Victory at Lincoln:** the spring 490 northern campaign has produced a major battlefield victory for Uther and Cornwall’s combined army.
- **Meredith born:** Meredith is now battleborn, with **+2 STR** and **Alert**, and is publicly celebrated as Uther’s granddaughter.
- **Madoc wounded and unstable:** Madoc survived, but his madness returned during the battle.
- **Ransom pending:** Liam/Gwinas captured a commander worth an expected **£173**, and Assterius/Wulfrun secured a bodyguard worth **£5**; Uther’s share and distribution remain unresolved.
- **Brangolet missing:** Millicent’s squire vanished during the elite Saxon foot-knight encounter.
- **Feast next:** Uther’s victory-and-birth feast is the immediate next scene.
