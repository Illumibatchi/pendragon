## Where we left off (end of [[Session 039 - The Veil, the Siren, and Merlin’s Release|Session 039]])
The company has awakened and freed [[Merlin]] from beyond [[The Veil]]. After hearing that [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] is consumed by [[Ygraine]] and the siege of [[Castle Jagent]], Merlin said “fuck,” took a horse, and rode toward Jagent to intervene or advise.

The party remains tied to the Jagent crisis. [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] has surrounded [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|Duke Erof/Gorlois]] at [[Castle Jagent]], and the siege army is still exhausted, bored, and demoralized. [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Earl Roderick]]’s loyalty to Uther has already waned; he is thinking about the [[House of Constantine|Bloodline of Constantine]], not Uther personally.

Far away, while Uther is occupied with Cornwall, [[King Ælle of Sussex|Ælle]] and [[Cissa]] have taken [[Pevensey]] after starvation and slaughtered its inhabitants as sacrifices to Wotan.

## Immediate fallout
- **Party location:** Returning focus to the siege lines around [[Castle Jagent]].
- **Merlin:** Awake, freed from chains beyond the Veil, and riding toward Jagent.
- **Uther crisis:** Uther’s judgment remains the central danger; Merlin may now confront, counsel, or enable him.
- **Roderick:** [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Earl Roderick]] is worried about the [[House of Constantine|Bloodline of Constantine]] if Uther dies.
- **Drustin:** [[Drustin]] survived fey-induced madness after the siren bargain, but permanently lost **2 APP** and now bears the visible aftermath.
- **Stone network:** The [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]] can open a blood-activated crossing through [[The Veil]] to the [[Plains of Ascalon]].
- **Saxon threat:** [[Pevensey]] has fallen in a massacre while British strength is tied up at Jagent.
