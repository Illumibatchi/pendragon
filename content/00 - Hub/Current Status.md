## Where we left off (end of [[Session 041 - The Feast at Caer Lloyw and the Poisoned Boon|Session 041]])
After Jagent, the company attends a feast at [[Caer Lloyw]] where [[Drustin]] successfully wins [[Duke Edaris of the March|Duke Edaris]]’s approval and marries [[Lady Rosalyn]]. The old Drustin/Rosalyn courtship obstacle is resolved.

The rest of year 491 leaves several new dangers in motion. [[Liam]] has sought [[Damsel Diana]]’s father in [[Summerland]] / the [[Mendip Hills]] and accepted a druidic boon as the price of Diana’s union: take a black liquid and poison [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]. Diana agrees to the bargain, but Liam and Diana are **not yet recorded as married**.

[[Millicent]] has taken [[Morgana]] into her household as lady-in-waiting and steward-in-training at [[Heywood]]. [[Morgawse]] and [[Elaine]] are also present in the political problem around [[Ygraine]]’s daughters, whom [[War Baron Ulfius|Ulfius]] and a Glastonbury churchman want kept out of Uther’s household. [[Assterius]] has married [[Squire Aspara|Aspara]], with [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]] sponsoring the wedding.

Far away, while Britain was tied up in Cornwall/Jagent, [[King Ælle of Sussex|Ælle]] and [[Cissa]] took [[Pevensey]] after starvation and slaughtered its inhabitants as sacrifices to Wotan.

## Immediate fallout
- **Party location/status:** Rest-of-year 491, shifting from court feast into manor/winter business.
- **Drustin/Rosalyn:** Married after Drustin reaches the feast Geniality target and wins Edaris’s approval.
- **Liam/Diana:** Conditional union/betrothal terms accepted; Liam now carries or is committed to a black poison meant for Uther.
- **Millicent/Morgana:** Morgana is in Millicent’s household and being trained for Heywood stewardship.
- **Assterius/Aspara:** Married, with Roderick offering major wedding patronage.
- **Manors:** Heywood, Yarnbrook, Coulston, and Edington all receive new improvements; Yarnbrook’s famine/depleted peasantry remains a concern.
