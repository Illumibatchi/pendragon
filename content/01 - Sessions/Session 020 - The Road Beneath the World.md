**In-game year:** 487

## Summary
At the opening of 487 the realm shifts again: Saxons retreat on some fronts, Linden is desolated, and rumors spread that the King of Deira has gone mad. With [[Excalibur]] broken into three pieces and [[Merlin]] missing, the conroi is pulled between courtly obligations and a desperate private quest.

Invited to accompany [[Uther Pendragon]] on his royal progress, the knights refuse and instead commit to [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]]’s charge to find Merlin and restore the sword. That refusal earns Uther’s suspicion (and contempt). Following thin leads through Pitum and back to Wells, they learn the truth: Merlin has “crossed over” and is no longer in this world. With [[Lady Wells]] revealed as one of the [[Ladies of the Lake]], the conroi chooses the only remaining path — a perilous descent into the [[Otherworld]].

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Realm state (487):** Saxons retreat; the King of Deira “has gone mad”; Angles abandon/desolate [[Lincoln|Linden/Lincoln region]]; the land is economically shattered. (Table talk + lore dump.)
- **Lore: Saxons of Berroc:** invited over by Constantine (Uther’s forebear) and remained loyal through Vortigern; serve as Varangian-style guards of the [[White Tower]] in [[Londinium]].
- **Choice:** accompany Uther on his progress vs pursue the Merlin/Excalibur quest.
- **Decision:** refuse Uther’s progress; pursue the quest.
- **Consequence:** **+10 Contempt** from Uther; the refusal is read as alignment with Roderick in a brewing civil conflict.
- **Command change:** leadership passes via a “glory off”; **Liam** becomes the conroi’s acting commander for the quest.
- **Pitum/Pitton counsel:** visit the home of Assterius’ father (ill, “plague”); learn Merlin hasn’t been seen since the Somerset blunder; he appears at pagan holy sites at **midsummer**; seek the [[Ladies of the Lake]].
- **Return to [[Wells]]:** the town is bustling again, but wary; the tavern goes silent at their entrance.
- **Audience at the manor:** meet [[Lady Wells]]; Millicent notes her white gown + tattoos like [[Lady Niev]] → suspicion confirmed: Lady Wells is one of the Ladies of the Lake.
- **Excalibur revealed:** the broken shards disturb her; she confirms Merlin is not “dead” in a mortal sense — he has crossed over.
- **Only path:** to reach Merlin (and mend Excalibur), the conroi must go where he is: the **Otherworld**. Lady Wells offers a guide.
- **Frome Stones:** travel to the spring/cave; enter with gear (no horses); meet the child guide [[Guido (The Guide)|Guido]].
- **The descent:** water runs uphill; the cave walk is **a fortnight**; supplies fail; Guido provides strange mushrooms; exhaustion + hunger.
- **Dream/vision sequence:** knights experience passion-shaped visions (Lady Ellen’s warning; Britain-personified confusion; death/doom imagery; a glimpse of the name **Arthur**).
- **The Abyss:** a vast chasm/waterfall where water “falls” upward; armor is lowered/discarded; rope descent with multiple near-falls; Guido is saved more than once.
- **Encounter:** **hobgoblins** (dark fae) attack; hard fight; Guido nearly taken; the conroi prevails.
- **Exit door:** liquid-rock stairs → iron door labeled **“Exit”**.
- **Arrival:** beyond is an alien enclosed hall/city with crowds, strange signs (“Exit”, “to London”), horseless smoke-chariots, glass/metal towers.
- **Hook:** Guido’s only guidance: find a **church with a golden angel**.
