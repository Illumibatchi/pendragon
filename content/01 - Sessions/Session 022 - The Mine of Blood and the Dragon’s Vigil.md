**In-game year:** 487

## Summary (1–2 paragraphs)
The conroi emerges from the underworld route and finds their squires and horses waiting **near Caerleon**—who insist only **~7–7.5 hours** have passed since the party descended. This contradicts the party’s lived experience and reinforces the growing sense of distortion around the Exit. The squires further insist that **Wells was burned in the war**, a claim treated as unreliable testimony / glamour rather than confirmed fact.

With grievous wounds now pressing on the surface, Liam hires a **chirurgeon** at his own expense. The conroi then turns toward the next concrete lead: the **copper mine near Caerwent**, where Merlin was first found. There, the mine proves to be a place where **blood and time do not behave naturally**—and where Merlin waits again, now behind crystal, under a dragon’s vigil.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Return near Caerleon:** the party emerges to find squires/horses waiting; squires insist only **~7–7.5 hours** elapsed.
- **Unease:** Assterius senses something wrong about the emergence point but cannot name it.
- **Contradictory claim:** squires insist **Wells was burned/destroyed in the war** (treated as distortion/unreliable testimony).
- **Chirurgery:** a chirurgeon is hired/retained immediately for the party’s grievous wounds (**paid for by Liam**).
- **To Caerwent:** the conroi travels to **Caerwent**, acquiring **ropes and ladders**.
- **Into the copper mine:** descending “Merlin’s hole,” the party is ambushed by small **green sling-creatures** on a ledge above.
- **Fight escalator:** spiked sling-shots that draw blood cause **new creatures to spawn**, rapidly multiplying threats mid-combat.
- **Discovery — healing blood:** contact with the creatures’ blood **restores vitality**; the party exploits this in the fight; **some blood is collected** for later.
- **Merlin again:** deeper in the mine, Merlin is found **encased in crystal**—preserved but unreachable.
- **The Dragon’s Vigil:** a **dragon / fiery lizard** guards the crystal prison; the conroi **retreats without engaging**, choosing to regroup rather than die in the depths.
