**In-game year:** 488

## Summary
After the victory at [[Calais]], the companions choose to remain and help reform the battered [[XX Legion]]. [[Uther Pendragon]] has apparently told the Romans that the British conroi are the finest strategic minds in Britain, and the Roman officers accept their direction. While [[Drustin]], [[Sir Madoc]], and [[Sir Gerry]] train the Romans around Calais, [[Millicent]], [[Assterius]], and [[Liam]] move into the countryside to restore the army's ability to sustain itself. Their operations drive the remaining Frankish forces south into [[Gaul]], and the garrison at [[Dunkirk]] surrenders on terms: safe passage if they leave arms and plunder behind. By the harvest moon, the Saxon Shore on this side of the Channel is back under [[Baroness Pomponia]]'s rule, and the XX Legion withdraws to its own territory, notably improved.

A messenger from [[Wildenburg]] brings news of murders and missing bodies. The town has imprisoned a young woman, [[Clotilde]], in the church and plans to burn her as the murderess after the priest and sheriff were killed. Inside, the companions find Clotilde still gripping a bloody candlestick beside the bodies; she claims the killings were done by the cubs of the [[Lord of Wildenburg]], wolf-headed men who walk on two legs. She says the angel Michael warned her that "silver shall keep the path safe and only the bravest shall defeat the darkness." The companions arm themselves with church silver, have a blacksmith fashion silvered spears, and hunt into the [[Wildenwald]], where they battle werewolves at an ancient shrine. The slain werewolves revert to human form, including the true Lord of Wildenburg. [[Drustin]] is bitten, and the trail leads onward to a strange [[Roman Manor in Wildenwald]] behind a high granite wall.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **After Calais:** The companions debate next steps and elect to remain in [[Calais]] to reform and retrain the dilapidated [[XX Legion]].
- **Training the Romans:** [[Drustin]], [[Sir Madoc]], and [[Sir Gerry]] train the Roman troops around Calais; each succeeds through a tested passion, trait, and skill.
- **Clearing the countryside:** [[Millicent]], [[Assterius]], and [[Liam]] lead operations to secure the countryside and restore supply. Millicent divides her 500 men into five units, with two led by valorous junior knights.
- **Frankish retreat:** The countryside operations drive the remaining [[Franks]] south into [[Gaul]]. [[Dunkirk]] surrenders; its garrison is allowed safe passage after abandoning plunder and arms.
- **Pomponia restored:** The Saxon Shore on this side of the Channel returns to [[Baroness Pomponia]]'s rulership. The [[XX Legion]] withdraws to its own territory by the harvest moon, seemingly much improved.
- **Messenger from Wildenburg:** A messenger reports murders and missing bodies in [[Wildenburg]]. The town has captured [[Clotilde]], a young woman accused of killing the priest and sheriff, and plans to burn her.
- **The church:** The companions enter the chained church and find Clotilde holding a bloody candlestick beside the remains of the priest and sheriff. She warns that the "Lord of Wildenburg" outside is the true danger.
- **Michael's warning:** Clotilde says the angel Michael appeared to her and told her: "silver shall keep the path safe and only the bravest shall defeat the darkness."
- **The hunt:** [[Liam]] deduces werewolves. The companions take church silver and have a blacksmith make silvered spears before entering the [[Wildenwald]].
- **Pagan shrine battle:** At the [[Wildenburg Pagan Shrine]], the companions fight three werewolves and two wolves. Liam kills a wolf and the final werewolf; [[Sir Geraint]] kills a werewolf; Drustin kills another werewolf but is bitten above the hauberk.
- **Aftermath:** The slain werewolves change back into human corpses, including the actual [[Lord of Wildenburg]]. One ordinary wolf escapes. A full moon hangs overhead.
- **The shrine and the trail:** The shrine contains a bloodied stone table and ancient writing that is neither Latin nor Greek. Two trails lead away: south toward town and east deeper into the forest.
- **Roman manor:** The companions follow the eastern trail and find a strange [[Roman Manor in Wildenwald]] behind a high granite wall, out of place in the forest.
