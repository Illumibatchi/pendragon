**In-game year:** 488

## Summary
Pursued from the werewolf fight, the companions take shelter in the courtyard of the strange Roman manor in the [[Wildenwald]], where the wolves refuse to follow. Exploring the site, [[Assterius]] identifies it as the [[House of Constantine]], marked by a recent inscription — *Lupis Macto* — and a charred wolf-man outline. In a ruined chapel, a radiant angel offers the company a trial of **Faith in God** or **Faith in Humanity**. The trial becomes a metaphysical gauntlet of virtues, passions, and temptations: [[Sir Geraint]] yields his name and arms to become the **Nameless Knight**, [[Assterius]] gives up his home and status to become the **Homeless Knight**, and [[Millicent]] sacrifices her own progress so [[Drustin]] can continue after his repeated moral failures. [[Liam]] solves the final riddle of chivalry by recognizing that chivalry is absent because he himself does not yet embody it.

When the trial ends, Liam uncovers the [[Holy Grail]] beneath the chapel altar. Each companion drinks; when Liam does, his cursed rubies spill harmlessly to the ground, seemingly cleansed. After burying the slain men of Wildenburg, the companions return to find Frankish and British banners over the church and guards acting under "King" [[Clovis]] and "King" [[Sir Madoc]]. Madoc explains he has struck a suspicious settlement with the [[Franks]] around Bayeux and Calais and claims the runed sword at his side is reforged Excalibur. Distrusting him, Millicent secretly swaps his weapon for [[Party’s Magical Sword (not Excalibur)|Fakealibur]] while keeping Madoc’s runed blade with the company. Back in Britain, the companions refuse to follow Madoc to Silchester, instead travelling to [[Wells]], where [[Lady Wells]] joyfully receives the runed blade and casts it into a still forest pool beneath the gaze of a stag. Most characters treat this as the true sword returned, but its exact identity remains uncertain because the conroi’s timeline/reality situation is still unresolved.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Roman villa refuge:** The wolves refuse to enter the courtyard of the strange Roman manor in the [[Wildenwald]].
- **House of Constantine:** [[Assterius]] reads the Latin over the archway and identifies the site as the [[House of Constantine]].
- **Omens in the ruins:** The companions find a recent *Lupis Macto* inscription and a charred wolf-man outline burned into the ground.
- **Ruined chapel:** [[Millicent]] discovers a chapel in the villa complex; a radiant angelic being appears and questions the companions' purpose.
- **The trial begins:** The angel offers a path of Faith in God or Faith in Humanity/Chivalry; the company enters a metaphysical test of traits, passions, and moral choices while devils pursue them.
- **Drustin tested:** [[Drustin]] repeatedly falters on lust, forgiveness, and devotion, especially over his resentment toward [[Bruce]].
- **Names surrendered:** [[Sir Geraint]] gives up his name and coat of arms, becoming the Nameless Knight; [[Assterius]] gives up his home and becomes the Homeless Knight.
- **Millicent's sacrifice:** [[Millicent]] reaches the end of the religious path but gives up her own place so [[Drustin]] can continue.
- **Liam's answer:** [[Liam]] solves the riddle of chivalry by realizing there is no chivalry in the world because he does not yet embody it.
- **Final choices:** At the trial's end, [[Drustin]] chooses the Child and [[Liam]] chooses the Cup/Grail.
- **The Grail found:** Back in the chapel ruins, [[Liam]] uncovers the [[Holy Grail]] beneath the altar inside a lead box.
- **Rubies cleansed:** Each companion drinks; Liam's cursed rubies spill away harmlessly after he drinks from the Grail.
- **Burial and return:** The company buries the dead of [[Wildenburg]] and returns to town.
- **Madoc and Clovis:** Frankish guards bar entry under the authority of "King" [[Clovis]] and "King" [[Sir Madoc]] until the two emerge from the church.
- **Suspicious accord:** Madoc says he has negotiated a settlement with the [[Franks]] protecting a new duchy around Bayeux and [[Calais]], while abandoning much of the remaining territory.
- **Sword swap:** Madoc claims the runed sword was reforged by Frankish craftsmen; [[Millicent]] secretly takes that runed blade and leaves him with [[Party’s Magical Sword (not Excalibur)|Fakealibur]].
- **Return to Wells:** The companions part from Madoc, return to Britain, and travel to [[Wells]] during a strange pagan festival.
- **Sword returned:** [[Lady Wells]] receives Madoc’s runed blade and throws it into a still forest pool watched by a stag; its exact identity remains uncertain, though everyone currently treats it as the true sword.
