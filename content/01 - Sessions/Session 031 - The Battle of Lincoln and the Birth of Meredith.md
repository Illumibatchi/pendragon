**In-game year:** 490

## Summary
In spring 490, [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] and [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] marched north together after their reconciliation through [[Merlin]]. [[Sir Madoc|Madoc]] commanded the conroi with the combined army until they met Saxon forces between [[Newark]] and [[Lincoln]]. The conroi struck for a high-value enemy commander: [[Liam]] unhorsed the commander and his squire Gwinas secured the capture, while [[Assterius|Asterius]] unhorsed a bodyguard and Wulfrun brought that captive back. Madoc was gravely wounded and stabilized by his squire Lewis, but survived and slipped back into madness.

After recovery, the conroi fought a small giant and two wild men. [[Millicent]], heavily pregnant, rode in wearing polished maternity armor to protect her husband. [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] charged down a wild man and tripped the giant, Assterius killed the wild man threatening Millicent, and Liam finished the giant. Madoc, mistaking Liam for Uther, hurled fashion-mad insults at the giant and survived again.

The conroi then tried to take ten well-dressed dismounted Saxon knights for ransom, only to discover they were elite spear fighters able to close ranks against cavalry. Millicent was unhorsed, Brangolet went missing in the chaos, and Geraint was nearly lost while trying to help. Madoc and Liam pulled the fallen back by the skin of their teeth. The fall drove Millicent into labor, and her daughter [[Meredith (daughter of Madoc and Millicent)|Meredith]] was born on the battlefield. The wider army drove the Saxons from the field, and Uther called a feast to celebrate both the victory at Lincoln and the birth of his granddaughter.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Spring muster:** Uther and [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] march north together after Merlin’s reconciliation, with Madoc commanding the conroi in the combined army.
- **Battle joined:** The army meets Saxon forces between Newark and Lincoln.
- **Enemy commander targeted:** Madoc chooses to strike an enemy commander and his picked bodyguards for ransom and strategic value.
- **Commander captured:** Liam unhorses the commander; Gwinas, Liam’s squire, secures the captive.
- **Bodyguard captured:** Assterius unhorses a bodyguard; Wulfrun brings the captive back to the rear.
- **Madoc nearly killed:** Madoc is badly wounded in the opening clash, stabilized by his squire Lewis, and falls back into madness.
- **Polished armor speech:** During recovery, Liam reminds the conroi the action succeeded; Madoc responds with a mad but morale-restoring rant about the importance of polished armor.
- **Giant and wild men:** The conroi is thrown into a fight with a small giant and two wild men.
- **Maternity armor:** Millicent enters the battle in polished maternity armor to protect Madoc and is struck by a wild man but stays mounted.
- **Giant slain:** Geraint charges down a wild man and trips the giant; Assterius kills the wild man facing Millicent; Liam kills the giant.
- **Domestic morale:** Millicent drags Madoc away, berates him for nearly dying again, and later restores morale with one of her signature slaps.
- **Elite foot knights:** The conroi attacks ten well-dressed dismounted Saxon knights for potential ransom, but the spear-armed enemy closes ranks and overwhelms them.
- **Near disaster:** Millicent is unhorsed, Brangolet goes missing, and Geraint falls while trying to aid her; Madoc and Liam prevent capture.
- **Meredith born:** Millicent goes into labor on the battlefield and gives birth to Meredith, daughter of Millicent and Madoc, named after Madoc’s mother.
- **Victory feast called:** The Saxons are defeated and driven off; Uther calls a feast for the victory and the birth of his granddaughter.
