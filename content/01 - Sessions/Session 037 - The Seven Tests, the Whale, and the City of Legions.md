**In-game year:** 490–491

## Summary
[[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] and [[Assterius|Asterius]] continued through the Loch Lomond vision-road, facing a sequence of chivalric and moral tests. They persuaded the [[Black Knight at the Well]] to let the peasants drink the lord’s water, avoided a wrath-test battle, passed through a gluttonous inn, survived an envy-laden feast in [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]’s hall, and chose toil over comfort at a fork. The choice brought them past a burning heart wreathed in thorns and left them feeling they had lost something priceless, even as they later found **£200 in gold** at [[Loch Lomond]].

Meanwhile [[Liam]] and [[Drustin]] tried to navigate the Cornwall crisis. They encountered [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|the Duke of Cornwall]] and his castellan riding hard; Drustin wanted to stop them, but Liam let them pass and narrowly interpreted his orders as seeking a boat rather than hunting a man protecting his wife. Afterward the two were caught in mermaid-and-whale strangeness: Drustin vanished by a moonlit pool, Liam was pulled under by [[Xorle]], and both men later woke naked in a bleeding, cushion-lined chamber inside a living creature. They escaped only when a whale spat them into the Irish Sea, where Geraint and Asterius—wintering with genial Danes and sailing south aboard the [[Knarr Kamelåså]]—pulled them aboard. The reunited men were dropped near the [[City of the Legions]], while [[Millicent]] remained absent/lost from the active events.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **The lord’s water:** Geraint and Asterius face the dark knight who allows only the worthy to take the lord’s water. Geraint proves his valor, while Asterius argues for mercy, justice, and generosity; the knight lets them through, and Geraint waters the peasants.
- **Cornwall passes by:** Liam and Drustin encounter the Duke of Cornwall and his castellan riding at speed. Liam fails to distract Drustin, but still urges letting them pass; Drustin objects, then follows Liam’s lead.
- **Merlin still guarded:** Drustin retains the hidden route to [[Merlin]] at the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]], with the warning that waking him is dangerous or forbidden unless the realm depends on it.
- **Campfire temptation:** Geraint and Asterius hear music and find a feast with naked dancing women. The test of lust is encountered, but the log does not clearly state a final pass or failure.
- **Taw River block:** Trying to return toward [[Tintagel]], Liam and Drustin find about 2,000 armed Cornishmen across the River Taw, apparently in place for roughly a week. They sneak through the forest.
- **Test of Wroth:** Geraint and Asterius encounter British knights fighting Saxons and Picts and choose not to join the fight, passing the test of Wroth.
- **Moonlit pool:** Liam and Drustin hear beautiful pipe music and find a moonlit pool. After Liam rests, Drustin and his squire are gone; Gwinas says he woke Drustin for watch and then fell asleep. Liam tracks Drustin’s boots and sword belt to the water’s edge.
- **Test of Gluttony:** At an inn, Asterius overindulges in exquisite food and mead. Geraint resists through devotion and persuades Asterius to stop; Asterius’ loyalty to companions carries him through, and they pass the test.
- **Uther’s feast / Envy:** In Uther’s hall, Geraint sees [[Lady Rachel of Carbonog|Rachel]] on Uther’s knee. Geraint and Asterius try to separate them; Asterius distracts Uther by flirting with [[Ygraine]], allowing Geraint and Rachel to escape. The test of envy remains uncertain, but instructive.
- **Xorle:** At the pool, a red-haired woman kisses Liam. Liam tries to resist through Loyalty to Companions, fumbles into melancholy, loses a brawl to [[Xorle]], and is pulled into the water.
- **The gilded man and the heart altar:** Geraint and Asterius meet a richly dressed gilded man offering the way of toil or the way of comfort. They choose toil, but the road shows them an altar with a burning heart wreathed in thorns; both feel they have lost something priceless.
- **Gold of Loch Lomond:** Geraint and Asterius reach a Loch Lomond beach and find a trading vessel with a chest holding **£200 in gold coins**. They feel materially richer but inwardly poorer.
- **Cushion Cave:** Drustin wakes naked in a small chamber of silk cushions with Liam lying across him. They realize they are not dreaming; when Drustin tears at the cushions, they bleed and a mournful sound rings through the creature. Acid later begins leaking in.
- **Asterius’ suspicion:** After the men are recovered, Asterius is suspicious and firmly believes Liam and Drustin hooked up in the Cushion Cave. This is recorded as Asterius’ belief, not confirmed fact.
- **Winter in the north:** Geraint and Asterius repair armor, winter with genial Danes, receive impressive helmets, and sail south aboard the [[Knarr Kamelåså]] toward Wales, where Geraint intends to marry Rachel.
- **Whale reunion:** In the Irish Sea, a giant whale breaches and spits two naked men—Liam and Drustin—into the air. They are brought aboard the [[Knarr Kamelåså]] and clothed by Geraint.
- **City of Legions:** The [[Knarr Kamelåså]] and its Danes drop the reunited party near the [[City of the Legions]], also called Deva or Chester. The city is known for tanneries, Daven cheese, Roman walls and roads, the former Legio XX Valeria Victrix fortress, a bishopric, the Cathedral of the Headless Martyr, a great amphitheater, a night-watching centurion ghost, and the mysterious Cheshire Cycle plays.
- **Rachel:** Geraint heads for the hills to marry Rachel; gifts ensue.

- **Treasure:** Geraint and Asterius find **£200 in gold coins** in a chest on a Loch Lomond trading vessel.
- **Gifts:** Geraint and Asterius winter with Danes, receive distinctive helmets, and gain recurring contact with the [[Knarr Kamelåså]].
- **Experience improvements:** Millicent improves Crossbow, Lustful, Energetic, and Stewardship; Liam improves Hunting and Merciful; Geraint improves Flirting, Energetic, Prudent, Generous, Merciful, Devotion, and Station; Drustin improves Recognize and Lustful; Asterius improves Compose, Religion (Greek), Merciful, Chivalry, and Loyalty (Companions).
- **Household/squire:** Millicent’s Father/Steward remains in service but is sickly after Winter Phase; Brother Nerovens remains; Brangolet, Wulfrun, Guy, Gwinas, and Geraint’s squire do not improve.
- **Horse survival:** Geraint’s charger, Liam’s Rabbit, and Asterius’ Troja survive.
- **Family/winter:** Millicent conceives a female child; Liam’s mother becomes sickly while his father is fine; Asterius’ mother becomes sickly while his father is fine and his child remains well; Drustin’s parents are both sickly; Geraint’s parents are already dead.

## Consequences & New Facts

- Drustin still knows where Merlin can be found, but waking Merlin remains a realm-level-danger choice.
- The Loch Lomond moral-test quest is compromised or at least unresolved: Geraint and Asterius gain gold but feel they lost something priceless at the burning-heart altar.
- Liam and Drustin survive the mermaid/whale ordeal, but the nature of Xorle and the living Cushion Cave remains unexplained.

- The active party members reunite near the City of the Legions.
