**In-game year:** 491

## Summary
The year opens with celebration and dread. At the [[House of Ector]], [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] and [[Lady Rachel of Carbonog|Rachel]] celebrate their wedding feast while news arrives that [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|Duke Erof/Gorlois of Cornwall]] is surrounded at [[Castle Jagent]]. [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] has ordered siege engines built and threatens to reduce the castle to ruins if Cornwall does not surrender. Farther east and south, the cost of that obsession is made plain: [[King Ælle of Sussex|Ælle]] and [[Cissa]] take [[Pevensey]] after a starvation siege and slaughter the inhabitants as sacrifices to Wotan.

[[Millicent]] completes her own road through the [[Loch Lomond Fountain]] visions. Offered a peaceful life with [[Sir Madoc]] in a castle above the sea, a village of happy vassals, and seven children, she remembers that she entered the fountain for her companions and turns back toward the beach. A white whale swallows her into the bleeding Cushion Room, and after being spat into the sea she sheds her armor, fights [[Xorle]] the siren, rejects faerie food and other temptations, and finally casts away the gilded tempter’s gold before a thorn-sheathed burning heart. She returns to the real world at [[Loch Lomond]], reaches [[Alclud]], and is carried south by [[Cnute of Kobn]] aboard the [[Knarr Kamelåså]], rejoining the company at Geraint’s feast.

After the feast, the reunited company reaches the siege lines at [[Castle Jagent]]. The army is bored, exhausted, and no longer fit for much fighting; even the knights and lords want to go home. Uther appears sleepless, obsessive, and dangerously unbalanced. The party avoids the king and reports to [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Earl Roderick]], who asks about the Chalice quest. They admit the spiritual quest failed and that Satan thwarted them. Roderick says the army seems to have met a similar fate, and his loyalty to Uther has plainly waned. His chief fear is now the survival of the [[House of Constantine|Bloodline of Constantine]], though he does not know what [[Meredith (daughter of Madoc and Millicent)|Meredith]] may mean for that question.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Castle Jagent and Pevensey:** The knights learn that Cornwall is trapped at Castle Jagent and that Uther intends to ruin the castle if Cornwall refuses surrender. While Uther is consumed by this siege, Ælle and Cissa sack Pevensey and sacrifice the inhabitants to Wotan.
- **Millicent’s impossible peace:** In the Loch Lomond vision-road, years seem to pass. Millicent and Madoc have a cliffside castle, a happy village, and seven children. Millicent chooses not to remain, remembering that she came for her companions.
- **White whale road:** Millicent chooses the white whale over a white lighthouse, tries to help it with whatever her companions left behind, fails Devotion to Deity, and is swallowed.
- **Cushion Room and Xorle:** Inside the whale she finds the red velvet Cushion Room; the walls bleed when poked and acid enters. Spat into the sea, she sinks in armor, strips it off, recognizes Xorle as a man-devouring siren, and wounds her with a dagger after drawing strength from Love of Family and her unborn child.
- **Otherworld temptations:** Millicent reaches a rocky beach, follows tracks, helps starving children open clams by giving them her dagger, refuses food at a strange naked inn, resists a bacchanal, and climbs toward a dawn-like mountain light.
- **The gilded tempter:** A jeweled man in silks offers friendship, reward, and the path home. Millicent takes a chest of gold after failing Generous, then sees a celestial heart sheathed in thorns, drops the chest, and kicks it down the hill; her deity seems to approve.
- **Return through Alclud:** Millicent emerges at Loch Lomond in the real world, reaches Alclud naked and muddy, is mistaken for a hill-person, and meets Cnute of Kobn, who was apparently told to expect her. Cnute gives her his own clothing and brings her aboard the Kamelåså.
- **Wedding feast:** Geraint and Rachel’s wedding feast unfolds at the House of Ector with the new feast system. Feast seating puts Drustin and Assterius closer to the salt, while Geraint, Liam, and Millicent sit farther away.
- **Liam and Ella:** Liam catches the eye of [[Damsel Ella of Caer Ergyng]], Rachel’s lady-in-waiting and seventh daughter of the Lord of [[Glevum]]. He woos her successfully and gains Adoration for Ella.
- **Whale interpreted:** Liam asks about the red-haired woman, whale, and pillow room. Friar Jonas says the whale signifies a test of faith and devotion that can set a supplicant back on the right path.
- **Dwarf at the feast:** Geraint notices a dwarf servant of the [[King under the Mountain]], sent to investigate the [[House of Ector]] and the political implications of the marriage. Asterius later toasts the dwarves and their lord to strong approval.
- **Millicent rejoins:** Cnute drops Millicent at the [[City of the Legions]]. She hastens to the House of Ector, bursts into the feast, hugs her companions, and rubs their faces in dirt from Pictland/Alclud.
- **Jagent siege lines:** The company reaches Castle Jagent. Siege engineers throw stones, but the soldiery is bored and exhausted; the army’s morale is poor.
- **Uther’s decline:** Uther watches from a vantage point and is rumored not to sleep or eat. The table reads him as having lost his mind.
- **Roderick and loyalty:** The party reports to Earl Roderick instead of Uther. Millicent raises the question of loyalty to king versus country, but fails Orate. Roderick’s Loyalty to Uther fails under a heavy penalty; he thinks in terms of preserving Constantine’s bloodline, not loyalty to Uther personally.

- [[Sir Madoc]] — Appears in Millicent’s vision of an impossible peaceful future with castle, village, and seven children.

- [[Damsel Ella of Caer Ergyng]] — Rachel’s lady-in-waiting, seventh daughter of the Lord of Glevum, wooed by Liam.
- [[Drustin]] — Has strong feast moments, talks up Liam to Ella, negotiates between quarrelling ladies, and later neglects Rachel while gaming.
- [[Assterius]] — Performs well at parts of the feast, toasts the dwarves and King under the Mountain, and continues under the burden of the Homeless condition and winter poverty consequences.
- [[Cnute of Kobn]] — A huge blond, Saxon-accented boatman who was apparently told to expect Millicent; gives her clothing and passage on the Kamelåså.
- [[Xorle]] — Appears to Millicent as a siren/devourer of men and is wounded in the sea.
- [[Uther Pendragon]] — Drives the siege of Castle Jagent and appears sleepless, obsessive, and unstable.
- [[Count Roderick of Salisbury]] — Receives the party, admits the siege feels spiritually compromised, and shows waning loyalty to Uther while worrying about the Bloodline of Constantine.

## Loot / Rewards / Glory / Notable Rolls
- Millicent fails Devotion (Deity) before the whale swallows her; later she succeeds Folklore to recognize Xorle, critically succeeds DEX to shed armor, succeeds Love (Family), and wounds Xorle with a dagger for 11 damage.
- Millicent critically succeeds Hunting while following tracks, fumbles Industry while trying to cover herself, succeeds Energetic to keep going, succeeds Temperate to refuse faerie food, critically succeeds Chaste to resist the bacchanal, fails Generous before taking the gold, then rejects the chest at the thorn-heart altar.
- Liam gains progress with [[Damsel Ella of Caer Ergyng]] and generates **Adoration (Ella of Caer Ergyng) 16**.
- Geraint gains/marks Adoration for Rachel and has mixed feast results, including moments of strong courtesy and a troubling flare of Hate (Fey).
- Asterius gains **5 Glory** for modestly telling of his deeds, succeeds in feast performance, and toasts the dwarves and King under the Mountain successfully.
- Drustin has strong social and gaming moments, including critical Courtesy/Just/Gaming, but fumbles Intrigue when discussing the southern adventures.
- At Jagent, Drustin and Liam fail Siegecraft while Asterius and Geraint succeed Battle to assess the army’s low morale and poor readiness.
- Roderick fails Loyalty (Uther) at an effective 7 after a severe penalty; his loyalty has waned.
- Winter poverty notes from the log suggest Asterius suffers a permanent disease/stat loss and Geraint suffers poverty-related stat losses, but these should be verified against sheets before final mechanical recording.
- Millicent’s family-yew shield and some gear remain uncertain: she assumes some equipment may have been lost, but the table suggests her squire may have kept key items.

## Consequences & New Facts
- Millicent is no longer missing after the Loch Lomond vision-road.
- The white whale/Cushion Room ordeal is now clearly a repeated spiritual test tied to faith, devotion, and a path back toward righteousness.
- The gilded tempter and thorn-sheathed heart show that the crown/Grail/Loch Lomond quest has spiritual costs still not fully understood.
- Geraint and Rachel are married.
- Liam’s new attachment to Ella creates a fresh courtship/passion thread.
- The King under the Mountain has an agent watching the House of Ector and Geraint’s marriage.
- Pevensey has been destroyed while Uther focuses on Cornwall.
- Uther’s leadership is now openly in question; Roderick’s loyalty has weakened, but he fears the loss of Constantine’s bloodline if Uther dies.
- Meredith’s status as Madoc and Millicent’s battleborn daughter may be politically crucial, especially if Roderick does not know or is not considering it.
