**In-game year:** 491

## Summary
With [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] still dangerously fixated on [[Ygraine]] and the siege of [[Castle Jagent]], the company chooses not to kill him or abandon the crisis. Instead, they leave the siege to seek [[Merlin]] at the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]], judging the realm’s need grave enough to wake him.

At the stones, [[Drustin]]’s Merlin-devotion reveals a ghostly, sleeping Merlin, but the company cannot reach him from the mortal side. Through trial, error, blood, and far too much fey nonsense, they discover that the Exmoor stones are part of a connected standing-stone network: tapping can carry between sites, and blood on all three stones opens passage through [[The Veil]] into the barren blue-sunned [[Plains of Ascalon]]. There, near the northern pool and thirteen-stone circle, they find Merlin asleep and chained beside [[The Siren at the Exmoor Pool]].

The siren demands **Beauty or Life**. [[Drustin]], [[Millicent]], and [[Liam]] refuse a life-bargain and instead create beauty: Drustin composes while driven mad by Love of Merlin, Millicent sings through Hate (Fey), and Liam seals the offering with oratory. The siren releases Merlin. Back in the mortal world, Merlin treats Drustin’s madness with an improvised tea, learns of Uther’s obsession and the danger to Britain, says “fuck,” takes a horse, and rides toward [[Castle Jagent]].

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Decision at Jagent:** The party talks through killing Uther, abandoning the siege, or seeking Merlin. They decide the realm’s peril justifies waking Merlin.
- **Road to Exmoor:** The company rides from Jagent through [[Taunton]] toward the [[River Exe]] and the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]].
- **Drustin attunes:** Drustin prays/communes through his devotion to Merlin/Merdwyl and receives the sense that they should wait.
- **Sleeping Merlin appears:** After moonrise, a ghostly Merlin appears sleeping above the stones. Geraint speaks of Uther’s madness and the kingdom’s danger, but Merlin does not hear him.
- **The veil problem:** Folklore suggests Merlin is on the other side of [[The Veil]], requiring the party to cross over rather than merely speak at the apparition.
- **Bad experiments:** The company attempts sleep, alcohol, mushrooms, song, prayer, and other improvised methods. Drustin sleeps through the night, wakes hungover, and the apparition is gone.
- **Northern pool:** [[Liam]] finds a spring and thirteen-stone circle about ten miles north near the headwaters; [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] enters the pool and, after a critical song, summons a beautiful siren who tries to pull him under.
- **Connected stones:** Tapping at one stone circle can be heard through another. [[Assterius]] and Millicent test heat and blood; blood on all three stones raises harmonics and reveals another world in the circle.
- **First crossing:** Millicent, Geraint, and Assterius briefly pass through into a barren version of the land under a blue sun, then return by putting blood to the stones again.
- **Entry team:** The party sends Millicent, Drustin, and Liam through at night while Geraint and Assterius remain behind.
- **Plains of Ascalon:** Across the Veil, the world is geographically similar but lifeless and blue-sunned. The entry team follows the dry riverbed north to the pool.
- **Merlin chained:** Merlin lies asleep by the pool, chained with a large lock. The siren says she did not bind him, but is happy to profit from the situation.
- **Beauty or Life:** The siren rejects evasive bargaining and demands something beautiful or living. The party refuses to trade a life.
- **Performance bargain:** Drustin composes, Millicent sings, and Liam orates. Drustin’s failed Love (Merlin) passion drives him mad, but his manic composition succeeds; Millicent channels Hate (Fey), and Liam’s oratory completes the offering.
- **Merlin released:** The siren accepts the beauty and releases Merlin as the sun rises.
- **Drustin’s aftermath:** Drustin pulls out and eats his hair, permanently loses **2 APP**, and is shaved by Millicent. Merlin orders “magical ingredients” and has Liam brew a tea; after about an hour Drustin calms.
- **Merlin briefed:** Assterius summarizes Uther’s pursuit of Ygraine and the danger to the realm. Merlin says “fuck,” takes a horse, and rides toward Jagent.
- **Next stop:** The year moves into Winter/experience rolls, but the next active scene is expected to return to the Siege of Jagent with Merlin now in motion.

## Consequences & New Facts
- Merlin has been awakened, freed, and sent toward Jagent.
- The [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]] can bridge the mortal world and [[The Veil]] when activated with blood on all three stones.
- The other side is canonically the [[Plains of Ascalon]]: a barren, lifeless, blue-sunned reflection of the landscape.
- The standing stones in the region form a connected network; tapping can carry between sites, but the full pattern and purpose remain unknown.
- [[The Siren at the Exmoor Pool]] remains an active fey danger and knows more about passage through the Veil than she revealed.
- Merlin’s original binder remains unknown; the siren claims she merely profited from finding him chained.
- The next immediate crisis is the Siege of Jagent with Merlin now entering the situation.
