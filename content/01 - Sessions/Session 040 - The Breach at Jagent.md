**In-game year:** 491

## Summary
With [[Merlin]] now returned to the Jagent crisis, the siege of [[Castle Jagent]] turns from exhausted stalemate to catastrophe. [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] is absent from his pavilion, the siege engines continue their useless bombardment, and the company splits between trying to restrain the army, investigate the locals, and find where the king has gone. In the forest north of the siege lines, Merlin is seen chanting over a sleeping Uther inside a stone circle; a magical fog and lightless darkness follow, and both Merlin and Uther vanish from the ritual site.

While [[Millicent]] prevents rash reprisals against local Pictish civilians and receives a sealed black vial marked with two yellow dragons, [[Liam]] follows [[Bruce]] and the monks into an increasingly alarming plan involving a barrel at the castle drain. Liam ultimately places and lights the barrel, breaching the wall of Jagent. The company charges the breach under archer fire, where [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] personally leads the defense. [[Drustin]], driven by jealousy over [[Ygraine]], fights Erof in single combat and kills him with a brutal critical blow. With their duke dead, the defenders surrender.

After the fall of the breach, Uther emerges from Castle Jagent with [[Ygraine]] and declares a new era of peace, justice, and security for his empire. The knights are rewarded **£25 each**. Drustin seeks to turn his victory over Erof into a path toward higher rank and marriage to [[Lady Rosalyn]], setting up a feast challenge where he must earn **7 victory points** with [[Duke Edaris of the March|Duke Edaris]].

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Stalled siege:** At [[Castle Jagent]], Merlin is initially nowhere to be found and Uther’s army is ineffectively bombarding the walls with poor siege engines. At the current pace, the walls might take a year to breach.
- **Roderick’s counsel:** [[Millicent]] asks [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]] how to counsel Uther amid faltering morale. Roderick believes Uther will not relent; the practical answer may be to quicken the siege or otherwise act around him.
- **Jagent identified:** The castle is attached to Ilchester/Lindinis and sits in a Pictish-dominated county whose people have largely adopted British customs while retaining their own subculture.
- **Bombardment resisted:** Millicent tries to force the siege engineers to stop firing. One crew stops under threat, but the broader bombardment continues under Uther’s orders.
- **Seeking local intelligence:** Millicent looks for refugees, expelled townsfolk, or prisoners. Locals are terrified of the royal army; after bribery, she is told to come to a bridge at midnight for an answer.
- **Uther absent:** At the king’s pavilion, the company discovers Uther is not there. A knight assumes Picts have killed him and tries to retaliate against local civilians, but Millicent and [[Assterius]] restrain him and prevent the attack.
- **Daniel’s report:** [[Squire Daniel]] says Uther went north into the forest with Merlin, wearing armor and taking no escort.
- **The bridge meeting:** Millicent and [[Sir Rolf]] go to the bridge. An old woman gives them a pouch containing a sealed glass vial, black as soot, tagged with two poorly drawn yellow dragons — Uther/Logres arms. Millicent keeps the vial.
- **Forest ritual:** Drustin, [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]], and Assterius track north and hear chanting. In a clearing with a stone circle, Uther sleeps while Merlin chants over him. They watch rather than interrupt.
- **Magical darkness:** A great mist descends at sunset. Merlin stops chanting; ordinary fire will not light, and even sparks fail. Drustin believes Merlin is the source of the darkness. When the company reaches the ritual site, both Merlin and Uther are gone.
- **Bruce and the barrel:** Liam seeks Bruce by the river and finds him with monks, including [[Brother Maynard]]. Their plan involves strapping Liam into a barrel and sending him toward a drain in the wall. Liam wriggles out of becoming the payload, but eventually moves the explosive barrel toward the wall himself.
- **Millicent redirects the inquisitors:** Millicent intercepts Maynard and later interrupts Bruce and Maynard interrogating Daniel. She sends Bruce and his followers north after the missing king.
- **The trail turns back:** The company in the forest sees horse tracks leading back toward the castle.
- **The breach:** Liam returns to the drain with Rolf, lights the barrel, and escapes under danger. The blast pierces the wall of Jagent.
- **Defense at the breach:** Cornish elite fighters, archers, a champion, and [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] himself move to hold the breach.
- **Charge called:** Millicent calls the company to arms and leads the rush toward the breach under arrow fire.
- **First clash:** Millicent rushes in to protect Liam and the companions, fighting unarmed and knocking her opponent down. Geraint, Assterius, Rolf, and Liam all fight through elite defenders.
- **Drustin vs. Erof:** Duke Erof rolls Love (Ygraine), while Drustin answers with Jealous (Ygraine). Drustin drops his defenses for a reckless attack, critically strikes, and kills the duke.
- **Surrender:** With Erof dead, the defenders begin surrendering.
- **Uther and Ygraine:** Uther leaves Castle Jagent with Ygraine and announces a new era of peace, justice, and security for his empire.
- **Rewards and next feast:** The king rewards the company with **£25 each**. Drustin hopes to leverage his success in Cornwall into higher rank and renewed consideration by Duke Edaris for Rosalyn’s hand; the next feast challenge requires **7 victory points**.
