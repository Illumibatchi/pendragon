**In-game year:** 491 (rest-of-year aftermath of Jagent)

## Summary
At [[Caer Lloyw]], the Earl of Malborough hosts a feast for his lord [[Duke Edaris of the March|Duke Edaris]], where [[Drustin]] tries to turn his glory from killing [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] into permission to marry [[Lady Rosalyn]]. With rival suitors circling and the whole table aggressively wingmanning him, Drustin reaches the required **14 Geniality** and wins Edaris’s approval; later marriage rolls confirm that Drustin and Rosalyn are now married.

The feast also introduces [[Ygraine]]’s daughters as a live political problem. [[Morgana]] asks after [[Merlin]], while [[Morgawse]] and [[Elaine]] are also present. A Glastonbury churchman and [[War Baron Ulfius]] want the daughters kept out of Uther’s household and placed safely; despite a botched intrigue attempt, [[Millicent]] takes Morgana into her household as a lady-in-waiting and later trains her as a steward for [[Heywood]].

During rest-of-year business, [[Assterius]] successfully courts and marries [[Squire Aspara|Aspara]], with [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]] offering to sponsor the wedding. [[Liam]] quests to [[Summerland]] / the [[Mendip Hills]] for [[Damsel Diana]]’s father, finds a druid, and receives conditional blessing for the union only if he accepts a boon: take a black liquid and poison [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]. Diana agrees; Liam accepts, confirming Diana’s long-running honeypot / “case red” thread but not yet making the marriage complete.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Feast at Caer Lloyw:** The Earl of Malborough hosts a feast for Duke Edaris after the late wars. Drustin’s mission is to impress Edaris enough to marry Rosalyn; the arrival of several unmarried great lords complicates the courtship.
- **Pevensey news:** While Britain was tied up in Cornwall and Jagent, [[King Ælle of Sussex|Ælle]] and [[Cissa]] took [[Pevensey]] by starvation and slaughtered the inhabitants as sacrifices to Wotan.
- **Feast challenge:** Drustin needs the party’s help and must reach **14 Geniality** to win Rosalyn. Drustin, Millicent, and Liam begin closer to the salt; Assterius and Geraint begin farther away.
- **Wingman offensive:** Liam, Millicent, Geraint, and Assterius repeatedly praise Drustin to Edaris and the court through speeches, courtesy, stewardship talk, and feast cards.
- **Rival suitors:** Leodegrance, King Pelinore, King Lot of Lothian, young Prince Uriens, and young Sir Ector all appear around Rosalyn with varying success. Pelinore makes a strong impression; Drustin counters directly and recommits himself to Rosalyn.
- **Drustin succeeds:** Drustin reaches **14 Geniality** with the party’s help and later completes marriage rolls. He is now married to Rosalyn.
- **Morgana appears:** A fourteen-year-old girl resembling Ygraine asks Drustin whether he is Merlin’s friend; she is identified as Morgana/Morgan. She also asks Geraint what he knows of Merlin.
- **Ygraine’s daughters:** A Glastonbury churchman asks Liam to help keep Ygraine’s daughters out of trouble. Ulfius has brought Morgana, Morgawse, and Elaine to find suitors and to keep them from remaining in Uther’s household.
- **Morgana joins Millicent:** Millicent offers Morgana a place as lady-in-waiting. Even after Millicent fumbles the intrigue, Morgana accepts because Millicent knows Merlin and is close to the “Chief Merlinist.” The churchman and Ulfius approve the arrangement.
- **Asterius and Aspara:** Assterius courts Aspara at Sarum, succeeds with Roderick, and Roderick offers to pay for the wedding — at least £500. Assterius and Aspara marry.
- **Geraint’s domestic season:** Geraint stays home with [[Lady Rachel of Carbonog|Rachel]] for domestic married life and heir-making attempts; no clear child result is recorded in the log.
- **Liam and Diana:** Liam travels to Summerland / the Mendip Hills to seek Diana’s father. Her father is a druid who blesses the union only if Liam accepts a boon: take a black vial/liquid and poison Uther. Liam checks whether Diana agrees; she does, and Liam accepts.
- **Manor improvements:** The company audits manor features and buys improvements across Heywood, Yarnbrook, Coulston, and Edington.
