**In-game year:** 491–492

## Summary
The session begins with Winter Phase and household business after the Caer Lloyw feast. Manor construction and stewardship rolls are made; several skills improve; the knights choose yearly living standards; squires and horses are checked. [[Millicent]] conceives twin girls, **[[Mabel (daughter of Millicent)|Mabel]]** and **[[Mildred (daughter of Millicent)|Mildred]]**, spending prestige to keep them healthy. [[Drustin]] gains [[Roger du Plain]], youngest son of [[Jarran|Sir Jarran of Du Plain]], as a squire.

Court gossip then shifts to the new royal order: [[Ygraine]] is now queen, pregnant, and at [[Tintagel]] with [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]; [[Sir Brastias]] reports hopes for a male heir; [[War Baron Ulfius|Ulfius]] says the northern lords are coming; and Uther plans to marry Ygraine’s older daughters to northern kings. In spring, [[Morgawse]] marries [[King Lot of Lothian]], and [[Elaine]] marries King Nentres of Garloth.

The company then hears that Sir Brastias has accused the player knights of treason. [[Millicent]] is not formally accused, apparently because [[Ygraine]] interceded, but she goes to [[Tintagel]] with the others. [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]] distrusts Brastias’s justice and sends [[Sir Elad]] the Marshal and [[Bishop Roger]] to advise them. At Tintagel, the accused are stripped of armor and baggage, confined separately but treated decently, and questioned by [[Dewi]], a monk. Depositions focus on Merlinist ties, the knights’ movements around Tintagel, contact with Cornwall, and whether they served a plot against the king.

The deeper charge finally emerges: [[Arthur]], the queen’s newborn son and heir, has been taken. Servants say [[Merlin]] appeared before Uther and Ygraine to claim the child “as per the agreement”; Uther first refused, but Merlin warned of a curse if the magic was not paid for, and Uther relented. At trial, Brastias reads the charge: **treason against the Crown, kidnapping the heir to the kingdom, and aid and comfort to the fugitive Merlin**. The trial is set for next session.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Winter construction:** Industry rolls for castle/manor works mostly go poorly, while stewardship generally succeeds. Liam succeeds Industry and critically succeeds Stewardship.
- **Experience and living standards:** The knights resolve experience rolls. [[Millicent]] improves Stewardship and raises Just by one as she pursues chivalric conduct. Millicent, Geraint, and Drustin live ordinarily; Assterius and Liam live richly.
- **Squires and households:** Brangolet remains with Millicent, though she confronts him about repeated absences; he points out the survival rate of present squires, and she concedes. Drustin receives [[Roger du Plain]] as squire. Liam’s new squire is the heir of the Baron of Warminster.
- **Horse notes:** Assterius’s charger Troja dies. Millicent’s new horse is clumsy; Drustin’s is affectionate; Asterius’s is resilient; Liam’s is mischievous. Routine horse notes are not spun out into separate horse pages.
- **Children:** Geraint has a blessed male child, later named [[Bruce (son of Geraint and Rachel)|Bruce]]. Millicent conceives twin girls, [[Mabel (daughter of Millicent)|Mabel]] and [[Mildred (daughter of Millicent)|Mildred]], and spends 1 prestige for their health. Drustin records [[Tessa (child of Drustin)|Tessa]] and [[William Whim (child of Drustin)|William/Whim]]; the latter is odd enough to be treated as a fey-baby note. [[Assterius]] and [[Squire Aspara|Aspara]] conceive a son named [[Leo (son of Assterius and Aspara)|Leo]]. [[Liam]] and [[Damsel Diana]] conceive a male child whose name is not yet recorded.
- **Royal gossip:** Ygraine is queen and pregnant; court gossip blames Merlin for the strangeness around Erof’s death and Ygraine’s claim that her husband visited her that night. Rumor also claims a son of [[Sir Madoc]] exists somewhere in the [[Forest Sauvage]].
- **Royal marriages:** Uther seeks peace through family alliances and gives away Ygraine’s daughters. [[Morgawse]] marries [[King Lot of Lothian]]; [[Elaine]] marries King Nentres of Garloth.
- **Treason summons:** Sir Brastias accuses the player knights of treason. Drustin is named as ringleader. Millicent is not accused, but chooses to go.
- **Brastias identified:** Brastias is Uther’s most trusted man and chief bodyguard: Christian, about 46, gruff, suspicious, brave, and loyal to Uther above all. His arms are a gauntlet on a green shield.
- **Roderick’s aid:** The company speaks with Roderick, who considers Brastias arbitrary and not truly just. He sends [[Sir Elad]] the Marshal and [[Bishop Roger]] for legal advice.
- **Tintagel confinement:** At Tintagel the accused are stripped of armor and baggage but keep swords and daggers. They are confined separately in the tower and told to remain in their rooms. Servants treat them decently.
- **Other accused:** A dozen or so other knights are also present; they believe Merlin has set them up.
- **Depositions:** [[Dewi]] questions the accused about faith, Jesus, Merlin, the alleged plot, what they did during the siege of Tintagel, whether they met Dubricus, and whether they had dealings with Cornwall’s household.
- **Drustin’s omen:** In confinement, Drustin prays through Devotion (Merlin). An all-white martlet brings a wreath of halm. Drustin feels answered, though not enlightened.
- **Millicent and Brastias:** Millicent seeks Brastias and bluntly insists the charges are false. Brastias says that without Queen Ygraine’s intercession, Millicent would also be on trial. He is unconvinced by broad “Britain” language but respects her bluntness enough to let an implied challenge to his honor pass.
- **Arthur taken:** Geraint hears from a serving girl that Merlin came before Uther and Ygraine to claim the royal child by prior agreement. The child is named [[Arthur]].
- **Trial called:** Uther sits flanked by [[War Baron Ulfius|Ulfius]] and [[Archbishop Dubricus]]. Ygraine wears mourning black. Brastias reads the charge: treason, kidnapping the heir, and aiding the fugitive Merlin. The trial begins next session.
