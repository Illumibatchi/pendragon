**In-game year:** 493

## Summary
Court at [[Silchester]] is dominated by the disappearance of the unnamed infant, the escape of Octa and [[Eosa “the Giant” Horsason|Eosa]], and growing concern about Saxon mobilization. King Pellinore attends as a special guest while pursuing the Questing Beast, reported somewhere between Silchester and Salisbury. [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] is distressed by the loss of the child and is openly seeking knights who will bring [[Merlin]] back for betrayal.

[[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Earl Roderick]] is sent north to [[Eboracum (York)|York]] as an envoy to broker an alliance between Logres and [[King Brodin of Malahaut|Malahaut]]. The Centurion-King's terms require Logres to send troops, provide its own food, avoid raiding Malahaut's farmers, and keep its knights out of York. The party proposes purchasing provisions, entering York, and receiving proportional aid, but the king refuses to amend the terms. The diplomatic outcome is not clearly settled.

On the route after the audience, a large Saxon force ambushes Roderick's party. [[Millicent]] is the only knight to detect the attack in time. The fight is hard-fought: Roderick is knocked down and targeted for capture, [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] goes down during the battle, and Millicent uses Brangolet to get Roderick onto her horse before returning to the fight. The party ultimately kills or drives off the attackers and recovers Roderick. No permanent player-character death or final injury status is recorded in the log.

## Scene/Beat Log
- **Court at Silchester:** Gossip concerns the Frankish wars and King Claudas's promised conversion. King Claudas defeated the Alemanni, while many nobles argue over whether war abroad is worth its cost.
- **Court news:** Octa and Eosa escape imprisonment. Saxon rejoicing and volunteer movement toward their strongholds follow.
- **Pellinore:** Pellinore is pursuing a fantastic creature and may have abandoned his kingdom to do so. A Questing Beast is reported between Silchester and Salisbury. Pellinore flirts broadly at court and appears to avoid Lady Nineve.
- **Drimant:** Millicent questions Drimant about his disappearance. He claims witches held him after the party left him unconscious in a ditch. His seafoam-colored eyes appear to ripple. He says he is present to chronicle Millicent's “fall.” Brother Maynard calls him a pagan. Whether Drimant is fey remains unconfirmed.
- **Syagrius:** Liam learns that the exiled, drunken King Syagrius blames Logres for abandoning Roman Gaul despite promises of loyalty and brotherhood. Liam insults him and walks away.
- **Uther and Merlin:** Uther is said to believe Merlin betrayed him and is seeking knights to bring Merlin's head. The nature of the alleged betrayal is not established.
- **Mission to York:** Uther tasks Roderick with brokering an alliance with the Centurion-King of Malahaut. Roderick asks the player knights to accompany him; Asterius effectively volunteers the party.
- **York audience:** The hall contains many Saxon delegates, including representatives from Nohaut, Deira, Sussex/South Saxons, and the Kentish shore. Malahaut's proposed terms are restrictive and the king refuses the party's revisions. The delegates appear predatory and pleased with the result.
- **Ambush detected:** Millicent succeeds at Awareness while Geraint and Asterius fail Hunting and Liam fumbles Awareness. A large Saxon force prepares an ambush.
- **Battle:** The attackers include Aetheling warriors, a Saxon knight, a mounted Heorthgeneat, Rich Ceorls, and medium archers. The party exchanges heavy blows with them and eventually kills or drives them off.
- **Roderick rescued:** Roderick is knocked down and targeted for capture. Millicent and Brangolet get him onto her horse; Millicent hides him and sends her squire to report his location if she dies, then rides back into the battle.
- **Geraint's squire:** A previously omitted squire is assigned to Geraint during the battle: Pip the Third. Pip's Homage roll to Roderick succeeds.
