## Notes
A baroness from Frankish lands associated with the Saxon Shore. She serves as regent for [[Lucius (Duke of Saxon Shore)]], but Lucius is not her child. She seeks political protection/allies against [[Clovis]] and the [[Franks]].

Pomponia is carrying [[Assterius]]'s bastard child, but **Asterius does not know this yet**.

## Timeline
- **(483)** — Appears at Easter Court with four-year-old [[Lucius (Duke of Saxon Shore)]]; Uther knights Lucius and makes him Duke of the Saxon Shore, with Pomponia serving as regent. *(Source: [[Session 014 - Easter Court at Sarum and the Duel of Sir Marius]])*
- **(488)** — Commands [[Calais]] under siege; coordinates a horns-and-bells sortie with the companions; rewards [[Assterius]] after the victory. *(Source: [[Session 025 - The Feast at Caen and the Battle of Calais]])*
- **(488)** — After the victory at [[Calais]], Pomponia takes [[Assterius]] as a lover. *(Source: [[Session 025 - The Feast at Caen and the Battle of Calais]])*
- **(488)** — The Saxon Shore on this side of the Channel returns to her rule after the companions retrain the [[XX Legion]], clear the countryside, and force [[Dunkirk]]'s surrender. *(Source: [[Session 026 - The Wolves of Wildenburg]])*
- **(488)** — Pomponia is carrying [[Assterius]]'s bastard child; Asterius does not know this yet. *(Source: [[Session 027 - The House of Constantine and the Return to Wells]])*
