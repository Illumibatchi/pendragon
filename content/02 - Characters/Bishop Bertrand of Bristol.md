## Notes
Bishop Bertrand of [[Bristol]] owns or controls [[Saint Brigid’s Flying Cauldron]] and guards it jealously. He is politically ambitious, using ecclesiastical authority as a route toward worldly power.

In [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]], the conroi secures temporary use of the cauldron by leaving [[Chest of Rubies (Liam)|Liam's cursed rubies]] as collateral. The cauldron is later returned and the rubies recovered.
