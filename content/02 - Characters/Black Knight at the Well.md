# Black Knight at the Well

## Notes
Dream/vision figure encountered by [[Sir Geraint]] and [[Assterius|Asterius]] after entering the [[Loch Lomond Fountain]]. He blocks battered peasants and denies passage to the top of a hill where a well waits.

## Timeline
- **490:** Appears in Geraint and Asterius’ fountain-vision road after the Duty/Pleasure split. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
