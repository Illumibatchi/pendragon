# Brother Nerovens

## Notes
Holy man from the [[Castle of Salt]] in [[Galvoie]] with chirurgery. [[Sir Geraint]] convinced him to join the quest by framing it as a Christian mission rooted in [[Archbishop Dubricus|Dubricus]]’ vision, [[Glastonbury]], [[Joseph of Arimathea]], and the missing [[Holy Grail]].

Brother Nerovens knows a tradition that one of Joseph of Arimathea’s descendants traveled into Cumbria and founded a kingdom in the mountains, strengthening the quest’s northern trail.

## Timeline
- **490:** Joins [[Millicent]], [[Sir Geraint]], and [[Assterius|Asterius]] at the [[Castle of Salt]] and accompanies them north on the crown/Grail quest. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
