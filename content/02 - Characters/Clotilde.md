## Notes
A young woman of about sixteen from [[Wildenburg]]. The townsfolk accused her of murdering the priest and sheriff and locked her in the church to be burned, but she claims the killings were done by the wolf-headed cubs of the [[Lord of Wildenburg]].

Clotilde says the angel Michael appeared to her and warned that "silver shall keep the path safe and only the bravest shall defeat the darkness."
