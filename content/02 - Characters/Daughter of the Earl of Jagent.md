# Daughter of the Earl of Jagent

## Notes
Beautiful young woman with long yellow hair seen by [[Drustin]] in a tower at [[Lindinis]] in [[Jagent]]. An oracle foretold she would be abducted by a dragon, so her father locked her in the tower for protection. Drustin notices the omen uneasily because his shield bears a dragon.

## Timeline
- **490:** Seen by Drustin during Uther’s muster at Jagent; he hesitates to approach her and suggests perhaps trying on the return journey. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
