## Notes
An unnamed elf (or elf-like) blacksmith encountered at the “Reawakening Fair” at Pengam Green (Cardiff, Otherworld). Repairs [[Excalibur]] and accepts commissions for arms and armor.

## Known deeds

- **(487 / Session 21)** — Takes commissions: coats of plates for the conroi; a combined axe/warhammer (“axehammer”) for [[Sir Geraint]]. *(Source: [[Session 021 - The Glass City and the Man in Vitriol]])*
