# Elvish Damsel of Appleby Forest

## Notes
Mysterious singing damsel encountered by [[Millicent]] in the forest near [[Appleby]] under the full moon, near a great white/silver horse. She asks whether Millicent knows where she is going and directs her south to the stream and west toward town.

## Timeline
- **490:** Encounters Millicent in Appleby forest and guides her back toward town. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
