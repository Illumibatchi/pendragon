# Fox-girl of the Forest of Gloom

## Notes
Reported by a local questioned by [[Drustin]] and [[Liam]]: a fox-like girl or creature knocked at his door, invited him into the woods, and fled toward the [[Forest of Gloom]] after he threw a horseshoe/cold iron at her. Reliability of the witness is uncertain.

## Timeline
- **490:** Mentioned as a strange local incident during Liam and Drustin’s return toward [[Warminster]]. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
