## Notes
King of the [[Franks]]. Enemy leader in the Gaul campaign culminating in the battle to lift the siege of [[Calais]].

## Timeline
- **(488)** — Leads the Frankish host besieging Calais; the siege is broken by a coordinated allied trap and surprise attack. *(Source: [[Session 025 - The Feast at Caen and the Battle of Calais]])*
- **(488)** — After the Calais defeat, remaining Frankish forces are driven south into [[Gaul]] and [[Dunkirk]] surrenders, leaving arms and plunder behind. *(Source: [[Session 026 - The Wolves of Wildenburg]])*

## Alias note
This character is commonly called **Clovis** in Latinized sources; the table is using **Hlodowig** as the canonical name.
