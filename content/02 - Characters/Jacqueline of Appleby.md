# Jacqueline of Appleby

## Notes
Daughter of [[Lady Appleby]], hidden during the household’s insecurity after raids by Gore and [[Malahaut]]. She seems eager to leave [[Appleby]].

## Timeline
- **490:** Introduced after the questers kill the [[Auroch of Appleby]] and feed the town. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
