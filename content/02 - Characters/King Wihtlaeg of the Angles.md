## Notes
Child King of the [[Angles of Anglia]], about eight years old when found at [[Peterborough]]. [[Sir Madoc]] believed an angel had commanded him to kill the King of the Angles, but the company persuaded him not to murder the child. Wihtlaeg was captured alive and brought back to [[Lambor Castle]].
