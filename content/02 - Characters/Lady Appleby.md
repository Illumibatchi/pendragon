# Lady Appleby

## Notes
Widow of [[Appleby]]. Raids by Gore/Gorre killed her husband, and [[Malahaut]] raiders killed her sons; by Session 035 she is living warily with her hidden daughter [[Jacqueline of Appleby]]. She initially fears the visiting knights have come to take her chickens.

## Timeline
- **490:** Receives the crown/Grail questers with suspicion, hides Jacqueline for safety, and benefits when the knights resolve the brownie/clover problem by killing the [[Auroch of Appleby]]. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
