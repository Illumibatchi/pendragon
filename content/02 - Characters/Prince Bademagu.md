# Prince Bademagu

## Notes
Elder prince of [[Gore|Gorre]]. During the questers’ visit to [[Gaiholm]], he is absent with Gorre’s fighting men, reportedly gone south to catch Saxons during planting season.

## Timeline
- **490:** Named as the elder prince leading Gorre’s absent army south against Saxons, leaving Gaiholm lightly defended. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
