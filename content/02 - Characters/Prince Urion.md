# Prince Urion

## Notes
Young chainmail-clad prince encountered on the road near [[Gaiholm]], seat of [[Gore|Gorre]]. He grants hospitality after [[Assterius|Asterius]] and [[Millicent]] present the shield found on slain Gorre men in the southern woods.

## Timeline
- **490:** Stops the northern questers near Gaiholm, asks their purpose, receives the shield of a slain Gorre bearer, and grants hospitality. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
