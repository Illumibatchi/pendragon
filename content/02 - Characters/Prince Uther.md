## Notes
The ruler of the parallel Britain encountered in [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]]. This world is ruled by **Prince Uther**, not the conroi's [[Uther Pendragon|High King Uther]].

This distinction confirms that the conroi has crossed not merely through time, but into a different Britain/worldline.
