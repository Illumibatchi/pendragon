# Silver-Haired Woman of Loch Lomond

## Notes
Beautiful naked silver-haired woman seen by [[Sir Geraint]] at [[Loch Lomond]], walking from the shore into the woods. Following her apparent path leads the questers to the [[Loch Lomond Fountain]]. Possibly connected to the lake, the [[Ladies of the Lake]], or the white-horse/naked-woman sightings near Warminster, but this is not confirmed.

## Timeline
- **490:** Appears near Loch Lomond and leads or lures the questers toward the fountain-and-altar clearing. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
