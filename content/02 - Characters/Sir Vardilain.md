# Sir Vardilain

## Notes
Knight/lord at or near [[Appleby]]. When the crown/Grail questers arrived, he asked why they had come and offered a practical bargain: kill the strawberry-eating [[Unicorn of Appleby]], let him keep the meat, and take the horn for their quest.

## Timeline
- **490:** Meets [[Millicent]], [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]], and [[Assterius|Asterius]] at Appleby and offers them the unicorn bargain. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
