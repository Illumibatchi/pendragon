## Notes
A beautiful, toothy siren at the northern pool near the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]] network. She values **Beauty and Life**, can bargain over passage through [[The Veil]], and knows enough of the other side to profit from trapped mortals and bound wizards.

She claims she did not chain [[Merlin]], but she was not above profiting from his imprisonment.

- **(491)** — Appears in the northern pool after [[Sir Geraint]] sings; tries to pull him under, bargains with [[Liam]] and [[Drustin]], and later releases [[Merlin]] after [[Drustin]], [[Millicent]], and [[Liam]] offer beauty through composition, song, and oratory. *(Source: [[Session 039 - The Veil, the Siren, and Merlin’s Release]])*
