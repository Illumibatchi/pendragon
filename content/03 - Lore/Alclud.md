# Alclud

## Notes
Small, underdeveloped town among hills on the northern boat route toward [[Loch Lomond]]. Its inhabitants speak a strange tongue, and [[Brother Nerovens]] judges them “not God’s people.” The questers do not linger.

## Timeline
- **490:** Passed by the northern questers en route to Loch Lomond. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
