# Appleby

## Notes
Northern destination on the crown/Grail quest route after the party’s Stafford and Giant Erge diversion. The questers continue toward Appleby while still ultimately following [[Archbishop Dubricus|Dubricus]]’ clues toward [[Loch Lomond]].

## Timeline
- **490:** [[Millicent]], [[Sir Geraint]], [[Assterius|Asterius]], and [[Brother Nerovens]] continue northward toward Appleby after surviving the [[Shrine of Hecate]]. *(Source: [[Session 034 - Erge, Exeter, and the Hounds of Hecate]])*
- **490:** The questers meet [[Sir Vardilain]] and [[Lady Appleby]], discover raids from Gore and [[Malahaut]] have devastated the household, bargain with the [[Brownies of Appleby Forest]], kill the [[Auroch of Appleby]], and meet [[Jacqueline of Appleby]]. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
