# Auroch of Appleby

## Notes
Giant cow/auroch eating the clover near [[Appleby]], starving the bees of the [[Brownies of Appleby Forest]].

## Timeline
- **490:** Angered by [[Assterius|Asterius]], mounted briefly by [[Millicent]], and killed by [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] after a chaotic fight; its meat feeds Appleby. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
