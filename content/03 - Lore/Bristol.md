## Notes
A port town on the Avon. In the parallel-world context of [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]], Bristol is politically contested between civic authorities and [[Bishop Bertrand of Bristol]], who is trying to turn ecclesiastical influence into temporal power.

The city is diverse, with a notable Jewish quarter and Iberian and North African merchants. Bertrand holds [[Saint Brigid’s Flying Cauldron]] here.
