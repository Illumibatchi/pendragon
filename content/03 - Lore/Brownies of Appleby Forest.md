# Brownies of Appleby Forest

## Notes
Seven small fey figures found feasting on mushrooms in the forest near [[Appleby]]. They respond well to courtesy, offerings, song, and restraint, but [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] warns that offended brownies can hound a household or family for generations.

## Timeline
- **490:** [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] and [[Assterius|Asterius]] win their attention with offerings and song, refuse their food, and accept their boon: stop the cow eating the clover that feeds their bees. Geraint asks that the brownies keep watch over [[Lady Appleby]] in return. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
