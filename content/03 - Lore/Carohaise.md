# Carohaise

## Notes
Settlement south of Stafford where the questers seek a bridge rather than risk swimming horses and gear across the river. Carohaise has also been attacked by [[Giant Erge]].

> Note: this may need later reconciliation with the existing [[Carohaig]] spelling if the table confirms they are the same place.

## Timeline
- **490:** The questers pass through while following Giant Erge’s trail and learn the giant’s devastation has spread beyond [[Stafford]]. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
