# Castle Jagent

## Notes
Castle where [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|Duke Erof/Gorlois of Cornwall]] is surrounded by [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]’s army. Uther orders siege engines built and threatens to reduce the castle to ruins if Cornwall does not surrender.

## Timeline
- **491:** Uther’s army surrounds the castle; the siege lines are exhausted and morale is poor. *(Source: [[Session 038 - The Wedding Feast, the Whale Road, and the Siege of Jagent]])*
