# Castle Jagent

## Notes
Castle where [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] is surrounded by [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]’s army. Uther orders siege engines built and threatens to reduce the castle to ruins if Cornwall does not surrender.

## Timeline
- **491:** Uther’s army surrounds the castle; the siege lines are exhausted and morale is poor. *(Source: [[Session 038 - The Wedding Feast, the Whale Road, and the Siege of Jagent]])*
- **491:** [[Liam]] breaches the wall with an explosive barrel at a drain. [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] dies defending the breach against [[Drustin]], and the defenders surrender. [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] emerges from the castle with [[Ygraine]]. *(Source: [[Session 040 - The Breach at Jagent]])*
