# Castle of Salt

## Notes
Royal seat of King Nym on the River Severn, also called Worcester. The castle is named for walls built from blocks of rock salt. The town is known for goldsmiths, leatherwork, and savory brown sauces; south of the castle, the Severn is tidal enough for ships to reach its quays.
