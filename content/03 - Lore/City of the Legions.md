# City of the Legions

## Notes
A major southern city (approx. 20 miles south of [[Wilderspool]]) with access to competent chirurgeons. Also called **Deva** in Roman usage and **Chester** in later/modern terms.

The city was once the permanent legionary fortress of the [[XX Legion|Legio XX Valeria Victrix]]. Little remains of Roman military organization besides roads and walls, but the city remains a walled seaport with a medium castle, an episcopal see under the Bishop of Deva, the Cathedral of the Headless Martyr, and the largest amphitheater in Britain. Local color includes tanneries, Daven cheese, the Cheshire Cycle mystery plays, and a ghostly centurion said to watch the walls at night.
