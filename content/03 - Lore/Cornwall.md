## Notes
Western duchy ruled by [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] and associated with [[Ygraine]]. After Uther's conduct at the [[White Tower]], Cornwall becomes the target of [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]]'s 489 campaign call.

## Timeline
- **(488)** — [[Duke Erof of Cornwall]] brings [[Ygraine]] to Uther's Easter feast, triggering the Cornwall crisis. *(Source: [[Session 028 - The Easter Feast at the White Tower]])*
- **(489)** — Uther calls knights to invade Cornwall, but the company chooses [[Sir Madoc]]'s call to the March instead. *(Source: [[Session 029 - Madoc’s Penance, New Manors, and the March]])*
