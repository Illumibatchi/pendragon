## Notes
A crown described by [[Archbishop Dubricus]] as capable of uniting Britain. It offers the conroi a sacred alternative to following [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] toward war with [[Cornwall]].

## Open questions

- Where is it kept?
- Can it restore the consequences of the [[House of Constantine]] for [[Sir Geraint]] and [[Assterius]]?
