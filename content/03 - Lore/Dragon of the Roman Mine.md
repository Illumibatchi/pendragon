## Notes
A dragon guarding [[Merlin]]'s crystal prison in the Roman mine near [[Caerwent]]. In [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]], it appears in human guise as a beautiful naked woman caressing the crystal.

When confronted by [[Sir Gerry]] bearing the [[Shield of St. Crispin]], it speaks in a dragon's voice: “I shall eat your soul.” It fights with claws and fiery breath, badly wounding [[Liam]] and [[Millicent]], but is slain by the company. On its death, Merlin's crystal prison melts away.
