# Excalibur

## What it is
The legendary sword of the High Kingship of Logres — a symbol of legitimacy as much as a weapon.

## Canon status / disambiguation
Campaign usage distinguishes **Excalibur itself** from the other swords that have been mistaken for it.

1. **Home-world Excalibur / the High Kingship sword**
   - Present at the company’s knighting in 481.
   - Later shown to [[Count Roderick of Salisbury]] in three broken pieces after [[Uther Pendragon]] strikes [[Eosa “the Giant” Horsason]] and fails to wound him.
   - Its true current identity/status is still tangled with the timeline problem.

2. **[[Party’s Magical Sword (not Excalibur)]] / “Fakealibur”**
   - Repaired by the [[Elf Smith of Pengam Green]] in [[Session 021 - The Glass City and the Man in Vitriol]] while the conroi still believed it was Excalibur.
   - Revealed by a parallel-world [[Merlin]] in [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]] to be **not Excalibur**.
   - Used by [[Millicent]] as the decoy blade in the [[Session 027 - The House of Constantine and the Return to Wells|Session 027]] sword swap with [[Sir Madoc]].

3. **Parallel-world true Excalibur**
   - Produced by the parallel-world Merlin in [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]].
   - The conroi refuses to steal it.
   - It belongs to the alternate/parallel Britain ruled by [[Prince Uther]], not necessarily the company’s home reality.

4. **Madoc’s runed blade / the blade returned to [[Lady Wells]]**
   - [[Sir Madoc]] carries a runed sword in [[Session 027 - The House of Constantine and the Return to Wells]], claiming it is reforged Excalibur.
   - Millicent takes that blade and leaves Madoc with [[Party’s Magical Sword (not Excalibur)|Fakealibur]].
   - [[Lady Wells]] receives the runed blade and casts it into the forest pool.
   - As of [[Session 028 - The Easter Feast at the White Tower]], everyone is largely treating this as the true sword returned to the lake, and [[Merlin]] reacts as if the matter is catastrophic. However, because the conroi’s timeline/reality status is still suspect, this remains **uncertain rather than fully confirmed**.

## Current status
- **Not cleanly resolved.** The campaign currently treats the returned runed blade as probably the true Excalibur, but the parallel-timeline problem leaves room for doubt.
- The only confirmed false blade is [[Party’s Magical Sword (not Excalibur)]].
- The only confirmed alternate-world true blade is the Excalibur shown by the parallel-world Merlin in Session 023, which the conroi did not take.
