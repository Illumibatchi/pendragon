# Exeter

## Notes
Cornish stronghold besieged by [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] during his campaign against [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|Gorlois]]. Uther’s aim is to kill Gorlois and take [[Ygraine]].

The town beside the castle was deliberately emptied before Uther’s army arrived, raising suspicion that Gorlois and Ygraine may have slipped away and the castle may be a ruse.

## Timeline
- **490:** Besieged for roughly six weeks by Uther; after [[Drustin]] reports that the castle seems lightly held, Uther orders an immediate night assault. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
