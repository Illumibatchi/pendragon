# Exmoor

## Notes
Region named in the hidden route to [[Merlin]], reached by taking the [[River Exe]] from [[Taunton]]. The [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]] are somewhere there.

## Timeline
- **490:** Named as the region where Merlin can be found at the Standing Stones, with a warning not to wake him unless the realm depends on it. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
