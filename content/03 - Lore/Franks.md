## Notes
A rising continental people/power. [[Assterius]]' research at [[Bath Scriptorium]] says the Franks take their name from their bearded axes, were not important in the Imperial age, and have been recently unified under [[Clovis]]. They are rebuilding after Hunnic devastation and are Uther’s target in the planned spring 488 campaign.

## Timeline
- **(487)** — [[Uther Pendragon]] calls for volunteers to drive the Franks out of [[Calais]] in spring 488. *(Source: [[Session 024 - The Feast at Sarum and the Forest’s Warning]])*
