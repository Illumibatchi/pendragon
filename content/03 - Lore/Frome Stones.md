## Notes
A spring-and-stones site where the boundary between worlds is thin. In 487 the conroi enters a cave by the stream here (water running impossibly “uphill”) and begins the descent into the [[Otherworld]].

## Timeline
- **(487)** — The conroi enters the Otherworld via the Frome Stones cave under the guidance of [[Guido (The Guide)]]. *(Source: [[Session 020 - The Road Beneath the World]])*
