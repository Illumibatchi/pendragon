# Gaiholm

## Notes
Seat of [[Gore|Gorre]]. The northern questers visit while traveling toward [[Loch Lomond]] and receive hospitality from [[Prince Urion]] after presenting the shield found on slain Gorre men.

## Timeline
- **490:** [[Millicent]], [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]], and [[Assterius|Asterius]] stop at Gaiholm, learn the fighting men are away under [[Prince Bademagu]], and secure a boat for the journey north. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
