# Galvoie

## Notes
Minor kingdom of Cambria embracing part of the central Severn Valley. Its wealth comes from salt flats mined and processed at Salt Town. The enchanted forests of Arden and Dean are expanding, increasingly isolating Galvoie from the rest of Britain.

Its royal seat is the [[Castle of Salt]].

## Timeline
- **490:** The crown/Grail quest passes through Galvoie on the road north. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
