# Giant Erge

## Notes
Giant troubling [[Stafford]] and [[Carohaise]]. Erge first ate livestock and then began taking people. His tracks lead northeast from Stafford, across a river, and toward the river’s headwaters.

The questers discover not one but two sets of giant tracks: Erge’s and an even larger set. Millicent asks why he eats people, and Erge answers that they feel good in his belly.

- **490:** The questers exploit Erge’s vanity, convincing him that a rival giant has insulted him and stolen his meals. Erge storms off after the second trail instead of fighting them. *(Source: [[Session 034 - Erge, Exeter, and the Hounds of Hecate]])*
