## Notes
Marshland region tied to major Christian and pagan legends. Near the Tor ([[Tor of Glastonbury (Ynys Wydrin)]]) and the Abbey of Glastonbury. Considered a “thin place.” Potentially strategically important if Uther seeks to secure a supernatural “gate” while campaigning against Summerland.

## Session 033 update
- Dubricus’ vision begins here: the fisherman takes the brilliant “crown” from Glastonbury before carrying it north. The questers also learn the [[Spear of Longinus]] and [[True Cross (relic)|piece of the True Cross]] remain at Glastonbury, while the [[Holy Grail]] was taken north about a century ago. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
