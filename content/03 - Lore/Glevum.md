# Glevum

## Notes
Home or lordship of [[Damsel Ella of Caer Ergyng]]’s father. Ella is the seventh daughter of the Lord of Glevum and lady-in-waiting to [[Lady Rachel of Carbonog]].

## Timeline
- **491:** Named as Damsel Ella’s family origin during Geraint and Rachel’s wedding feast. *(Source: [[Session 038 - The Wedding Feast, the Whale Road, and the Siege of Jagent]])*
