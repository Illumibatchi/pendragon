# Gore

## Notes
Northern land/faction associated at table with Bagdemagus. Around [[Appleby]], Lady Appleby fears visitors from Gore/Gorre because raiders from there killed her husband. Spelling in player notes varies between Gore, Gorre, and Gorr; this page uses **Gore** as the clean vault form.

## Timeline
- **490:** [[Lady Appleby]] says raiders from Gore killed her husband; [[Millicent]] later finds a shield tied to Gore among decomposing knights hanging in the forest. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
- **490:** [[Gaiholm]] is identified as Gorre’s seat; the questers learn the fighting men are away under [[Prince Bademagu|Bademagu]] and that Uther previously fought Gorre but was sent back south. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
