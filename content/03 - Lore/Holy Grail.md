## Notes
Christian tradition: vessel used to collect Christ’s blood, brought to Britain by [[Joseph of Arimathea]]. Mentioned in connection with [[Glastonbury]] and the [[Abbey of Glastonbury]].

In Session 027, [[Liam]] discovers the Grail buried beneath the altar in the [[House of Constantine]]. After the companions drink from it, Liam's cursed rubies spill harmlessly away, suggesting the relic has cleansed him of their influence.
