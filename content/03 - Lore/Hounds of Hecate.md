# Hounds of Hecate

## Notes
Soul-devouring supernatural hounds bound to the ritual at the [[Shrine of Hecate]]. The first hound encountered has an iron-hard hide, strikes with terrifying force, and vanishes in fire and brimstone when defeated.

## Timeline
- **490:** After [[Millicent]] fails one part of the maiden’s trial at the [[Shrine of Hecate]], a Hound of Hecate appears and badly wounds the party before [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] lands the decisive blow. *(Source: [[Session 034 - Erge, Exeter, and the Hounds of Hecate]])*
