## Notes
A strange Roman villa site deep in the [[Wildenwald]] where pursuing wolves refuse to enter the courtyard. The arch identifies it as the **House of Constantine**, and the ruins contain a chapel, a recent *Lupis Macto* inscription, a charred wolf-man outline, and an angelic trial site.

The companions discover the [[Holy Grail]] buried beneath the chapel altar here.

## Timeline
- **(488)** — After slaying the werewolves of [[Wildenburg]], the companions flee to the villa courtyard, identify it as the [[House of Constantine]], survive an angelic trial of faith/chivalry, and uncover the [[Holy Grail]] beneath the altar. *(Source: [[Session 027 - The House of Constantine and the Return to Wells]])*

- **491:** [[Count Roderick of Salisbury|Roderick]] worries that if [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] dies the Bloodline of Constantine may be lost, not knowing or not considering [[Meredith (daughter of Madoc and Millicent)|Meredith]]’s significance. *(Source: [[Session 038 - The Wedding Feast, the Whale Road, and the Siege of Jagent]])*
