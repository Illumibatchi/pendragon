# House of Ector

## Notes
Household of [[Sir Ector]] and the setting for [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] and [[Lady Rachel of Carbonog|Rachel]]’s wedding feast. A dwarf agent of the [[King under the Mountain]] attends or observes the feast, concerned with the political implications of the marriage.

## Timeline
- **491:** Hosts Geraint and Rachel’s wedding feast, where [[Millicent]] rejoins the party after her Loch Lomond ordeal. *(Source: [[Session 038 - The Wedding Feast, the Whale Road, and the Siege of Jagent]])*
