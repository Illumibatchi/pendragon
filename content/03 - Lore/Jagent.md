# Jagent

## Notes
Pictish-dominated county on the border of Logres and [[Cornwall]]. Its Pictish population was brought south by Vortigern to serve as a buffer against Irish raiders; the “Jagent Picts” have largely adopted British customs while remaining a distinct local subculture.

Its county seat is [[Lindinis]], also called Ilchester.
