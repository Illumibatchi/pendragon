# King under the Mountain

## Notes
A subterranean or fae lord whose dwarf servant attends Geraint and Rachel’s wedding feast to learn what is happening in the [[House of Ector]] and what political implications the marriage may have. The dwarf mentions hunting large bugs beneath the mountain.
