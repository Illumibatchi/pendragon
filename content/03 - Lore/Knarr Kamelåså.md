# Knarr Kamelåså

## Notes
A Danish knarr / party boat that carries [[Sir Geraint|Geraint]] and [[Assterius|Asterius]] south after they winter with genial Danes. When a whale breaches in the Irish Sea and spits naked [[Liam]] and [[Drustin]] into the air, the men are hauled aboard the Kamelåså and clothed by Geraint.

**Game Note:** Have the knarr Kamelåså appear in every episode.

## Timeline
- **(490–491 / Winter)** — Carries Geraint and Asterius south, recovers Liam and Drustin from the whale incident, and drops the reunited party near the [[City of the Legions]]. *(Source: [[Session 037 - The Seven Tests, the Whale, and the City of Legions]])*
