## Notes
A druidic religious order often associated with the “women in white.” Sometimes “**the** Lady of the Lake” is used as a definite title (high priestess), while there are multiple Ladies of the Lake in the order.

For earlier rumor/context notes, see: [[Ladies of the Lake (rumor)]].

## Confirmed appearances
- [[Lady Wells]] (Wells) is one of the order. *(Source: [[Session 020 - The Road Beneath the World]])*
