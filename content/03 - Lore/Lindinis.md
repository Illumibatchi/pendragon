# Lindinis

## Notes
Large motte-and-bailey castle and market town, also called Ilchester, serving as the county seat of [[Jagent]]. The market town retains well-built Roman walls.

A separate tower attached to the keep by a bridge holds the [[Daughter of the Earl of Jagent]], locked away because of a dragon-abduction prophecy.

## Timeline
- **490:** Uther’s army musters here before the march to [[Exeter]]. *(Source: [[Session 033 - The Crown Quest, the Siege of Exeter, and the Giant Erge]])*
