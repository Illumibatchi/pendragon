# Loch Lomond Fountain

## Notes
Supernatural fountain-and-altar clearing near [[Loch Lomond]], reached after the questers follow the [[Silver-Haired Woman of Loch Lomond]]. The site includes white marble tiles, a fountain with no visible bottom or jet, and a seven-stepped pyramid or ziggurat with a stained altar and body-shaped chest indentation.

[[Love-Sun Water]] from the fountain cleanses and heals the altar when poured into the indentation. The fountain also shows visions of the viewer’s life and can draw people into dream/death spaces.
