# Loch Lomond

## Notes
Northern lake identified by [[Assterius|Asterius]] as the likely “elm-shaded lake” from [[Archbishop Dubricus|Dubricus]]’ vision. The clue comes through the River Leven, whose name means something like “Elm-water,” flowing into Loch Lomond.

The lake lies far north, beyond familiar Logres roads and near the path implied by forests, mountains, and two walls.
