# Love-Sun Water

## Notes
Shining water from the [[Loch Lomond Fountain]]. When [[Assterius|Asterius]] drinks it, it feels as though it contains love and reveals sunlight within the fountain. When poured onto the altar indentation, it turns the brown stain red, washes it away, and heals the stone. It is then used in [[Sir Geraint]]’s full-body baptism.

## Timeline
- **490:** Discovered and used at Loch Lomond during the attempt to cleanse or restore Geraint. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
