## Notes
The sword carried by the conroi after the Otherworld repair was believed to be [[Excalibur]], but [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]] reveals otherwise. It glows blue in the presence of the woman/dragon in the crystal chamber, but when the freed parallel-world [[Merlin]] sees it, he says it is **not** Excalibur and produces the true Excalibur from his robes.

The sword is still magical and important, but its true name, origin, and purpose remain unknown. The exact Old English name was not captured reliably in the transcript and should be filled in from Foundry chat if available.
