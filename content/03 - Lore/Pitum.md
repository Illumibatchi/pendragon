## Notes
Home of Assterius’ father (visited in 487 while seeking leads on [[Merlin]] and the [[Ladies of the Lake]]). Assterius’ father was ill and could not ride to [[Londinium]] with [[Uther Pendragon]].

## Timeline
- **(487)** — The conroi visits Pitum to consult Assterius’ father about Merlin; learns Merlin appears at pagan holy sites (midsummer) and that the Ladies of the Lake may know more. *(Source: [[Session 020 - The Road Beneath the World]])*
