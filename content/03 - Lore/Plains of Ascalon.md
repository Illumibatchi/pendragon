# Plains of Ascalon

## Notes
The blue-sunned, lifeless other side reached through [[The Veil]] at the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]]. The geography mirrors the mortal landscape, but there is no ordinary water, greenery, or animal life.

Near the reflection of the northern pool and thirteen-stone circle, the company found [[Merlin]] asleep and chained beside [[The Siren at the Exmoor Pool]].
