## Notes
The Roman/copper mine near [[Caerwent]] where the conroi first found [[Merlin]] encased in crystal and guarded by the [[Dragon of the Roman Mine]].

In [[Session 022 - The Mine of Blood and the Dragon’s Vigil]], the mine also contains green sling-creatures whose blood has healing properties and whose attacks spawn more creatures when they draw blood.

In [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]], the conroi returns with the [[Shield of St. Crispin]], confronts the dragon in human guise, slays it, and frees the parallel-world Merlin from crystal.
