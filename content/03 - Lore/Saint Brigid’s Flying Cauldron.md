## Notes
A miraculous cauldron associated with Saint Brigid. It can turn bathwater into beer and, more importantly for the conroi, fly through the air while carrying up to six armored knights. It does not carry horses or squires.

In [[Session 023 - The Dragon Beneath the Mine]], research at [[Bath Scriptorium]] reveals the relic is held by [[Bishop Bertrand of Bristol]]. The conroi borrows it by leaving [[Chest of Rubies (Liam)|Liam's cursed rubies]] as collateral, uses it to solve the time/distance problem around Downton and the mine, then returns it and recovers the rubies.
