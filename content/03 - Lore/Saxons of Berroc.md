## Notes
A clan of Saxons invited to Britain by Constantine (Uther’s forebear) to fight the Picts and hillmen after the Romans left. They remained loyal to Logres through the Vortigern-era betrayals and still serve Uther.

Described as a Varangian-style guard force for the [[White Tower]] in [[Londinium]].
