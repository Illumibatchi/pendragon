# Shrine of Hecate

## Notes
Mountain cave-shrine found by the northern questers while following [[Giant Erge]] by night. Under the full moon, they find statues of maiden, mother, and crone, each marked with moon pendants.

A Greek inscription describes a ritual of supplication before the Triple Goddess. Each phase requires three skills, two traits, and a passion. Success promises a reward; failure summons the [[Hounds of Hecate]] to devour the supplicant’s soul.

## Timeline
- **490:** [[Millicent]] attempts the maiden’s trial, succeeds with dancing and loyalty, but guesses one trait incorrectly. A Hound of Hecate appears; the party defeats it, and the ritual is deferred. *(Source: [[Session 034 - Erge, Exeter, and the Hounds of Hecate]])*
