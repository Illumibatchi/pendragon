# Standing Stones of Exmoor

## Notes
Standing stones in [[Exmoor]] where the maidens on the road to [[Wells]] say [[Merlin]] can be found. They warn [[Drustin]] not to wake him unless the realm depends upon it.

In 491, the company discovers the stones are part of a broader connected standing-stone network. Tapping can carry between sites, and blood on all three Exmoor stones raises harmonics and opens passage through [[The Veil]] to the [[Plains of Ascalon]].

## Timeline
- **490:** Revealed to Drustin as Merlin’s location after he proves his devotion and loyalty to Merlin/Merdwyl. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*

- **491:** [[Drustin]] attunes to Merlin at the stones; the company sees Merlin as a sleeping ghostly figure, then uses blood on the three stones to cross the Veil and free him. *(Source: [[Session 039 - The Veil, the Siren, and Merlin’s Release]])*
