## Notes
Ancient monument used for high-status royal burial. In 490, [[Sir Madoc]] is buried here beside his uncle, likely [[Aurelius]].

## Timeline
- **(490)** — [[Sir Madoc]] is buried at Stonehenge beside his uncle, likely [[Aurelius]], after dying from wounds taken at [[Lincoln]]. *(Source: [[Session 032 - Madoc’s Funeral and the Question of Britain’s Crown]])*
- **490:** [[Millicent]] appears at Stonehenge in a fountain vision or death-space with [[Aurelius]]. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
