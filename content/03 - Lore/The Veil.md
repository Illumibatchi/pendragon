# The Veil

## Notes
Boundary between the mortal world and a ghostly/otherworldly side. At the [[Standing Stones of Exmoor]], blood on all three stones raises harmonics and opens passage through the Veil to the [[Plains of Ascalon]].

From the mortal side, [[Merlin]] appeared as a sleeping ghostly figure above the stones and could not hear the company. Crossing the Veil allowed the party to find him physically chained near the northern pool.
