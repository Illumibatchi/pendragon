# Tintagel

## Notes
Cornish sea-cliff stronghold and likely refuge of [[Duke Erof of Cornwall|Gorlois]] and [[Ygraine]] after [[Exeter]] proved lightly defended and empty of its true prizes.

## Timeline
- **490:** After Exeter falls, [[Uther Pendragon|Uther]] identifies Tintagel as Gorlois and Ygraine’s likely refuge and marches his army there as autumn begins. *(Source: [[Session 034 - Erge, Exeter, and the Hounds of Hecate]])*
- **490:** Cornish forces sally to disrupt Uther’s siege preparations; the fighting is inconclusive but Cornwall is forced back into Tintagel and Uther’s siege begins in earnest. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
- **490:** [[Liam]] and [[Drustin]] consider acquiring a boat to inspect Tintagel’s seaward cliffs and later infer that the army likely circled back toward Tintagel. *(Source: [[Session 036 - The Sun-Water of Loch Lomond and the Road to Merlin]])*
