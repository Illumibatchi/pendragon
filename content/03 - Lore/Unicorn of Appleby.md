# Unicorn of Appleby

## Notes
Unicorn near [[Appleby]] said by [[Sir Vardilain]] to be eating all the strawberries. Vardilain offers the questers the horn if they kill it and let him keep the meat.

## Timeline
- **490:** Offered as a possible path to a unicorn horn for the crown/Grail questers’ spiritual/healing errand. *(Source: [[Session 035 - The Battle of Tintagel and the Brownies of Appleby]])*
