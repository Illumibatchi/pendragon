## Notes
Wolf-headed men associated with the [[Lord of Wildenburg]]. [[Clotilde]] calls them his "cubs" and says they killed the priest and sheriff of [[Wildenburg]].

Silver is believed to be important against them, based on Clotilde's warning from Michael. The companions fight three werewolves and two wolves at the [[Wildenburg Pagan Shrine]]. The slain werewolves revert to human form after death.

- **(488)** - The companions kill three werewolves at the [[Wildenburg Pagan Shrine]]; one turns out to be the [[Lord of Wildenburg]]. [[Drustin]] is bitten during the battle. *(Source: [[Session 026 - The Wolves of Wildenburg]])*
