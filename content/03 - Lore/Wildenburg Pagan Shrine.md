## Notes
An ancient pagan shrine in the [[Wildenwald]]. It contains a central stone table with blood on it and writing that is neither Latin nor Greek. The script appears ancient.

Two trails lead from the shrine: one south toward [[Wildenburg]], and one east deeper into the forest toward the [[Roman Manor in Wildenwald]].
