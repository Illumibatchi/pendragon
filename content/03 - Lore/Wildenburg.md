## Notes
A town near the post-Calais campaign route in Gaul. A messenger summons the companions after repeated murders and disappearances; the townsfolk have imprisoned [[Clotilde]] in the church and intend to burn her as the murderess.

The crisis points toward the [[Lord of Wildenburg]], werewolves in the nearby [[Wildenwald]], and an ancient [[Wildenburg Pagan Shrine]].
