## Notes
Salisbury manor granted to [[Drustin]] when the company becomes vassal knights. It has a strong lead-roofed hall, a deer park, and a pagan barrow, but is in severe hardship after war losses and famine.

## Features
- lead-roofed hall (+defensive value)
- deer park
- pagan barrow
- starving families / depleted peasantry

## Timeline
- **(488 / Winter)** — Granted to [[Drustin]] as part of the company's elevation to landed vassal knights of Salisbury. *(Source: [[Session 029 - Madoc’s Penance, New Manors, and the March]])*
